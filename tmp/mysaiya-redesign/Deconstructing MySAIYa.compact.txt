Deconstructing MySAIYa Introduction MySAIYa is an acronym for Malaysian Sai Young Adults and represents the spirited youth wing of the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM). As the moniker "MySAIYa'' is conceived, careful considerations were deliberated upon to ensure the name fulﬁls our emergent need to adopt a distinctive yet harmonious, eﬀective, and uniﬁed perspective that is both modern and culturally signiﬁcant to Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, SSIOM's values and the Malaysian society as a whole also resonating with a modern audience. As we go into the depth of MySAIYa, one will realise that with this new name, we stand at the intersection of personal identity, cultural heritage, and spiritual aspiration. This name reﬂects who we are, where we come from, and where we aim to go. It is a call to action, an invitation to join hands, hearts, and minds in a journey of self-discovery, community service, and spiritual growth. In MySAIYa, we ﬁnd a mirror of ourselves, our collective Malaysian spirit, and our shared human quest for meaning and connection.

MySAIYa Logo The MySAIYa logo blends traditional and modern elements, reﬂecting our core values and aspirations for the future. At the logo's centre is the iconic silhouette of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, symbolising the guiding light of His teachings. The silhouette serves as a reminder and calls for introspection and the pursuit of spirituality. The script "ya" surrounding the silhouette represents the energy and dynamism of young adults in their journey towards discovering their true selves while staying true to their spiritual quest. The circular boundary represents the sense of community and unity that transcends borders, where every young adult and their limitless potential are valued. Inspired by the SSSIO/ SSIOM logo, the colour scheme represents unity and strength as a global community. The rich navy blue and warm orange colours symbolise the balance between depth of purpose and passion for action. The logo represents a community rooted in profound spiritual teachings yet embracing personal growth and societal contribution.

"SAI" in MySAIYa serves a quadfold purpose: 1. Sai in honour of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba and a commitment to His teachings. 2. Sai, our inner quest, i.e. SAI- "Seek Always Inside", our spiritual mission with a service outlook. 3. Sai is the triad of transformation we aim for, i.e. SAI- Spiritual Evolution, Association Elevation and Individual Transformation 4. Sai signiﬁes our methods, i.e. SAI- Service (Karma Yoga), Adoration (Bhakti Yoga), and Illumination (Jnana Yoga). The second portion of MySAIYa, following the preﬁx My, is SAI, written in capital letters, encompassing the mission and core principles of the teachings we strive to uphold. The acronym "SAI" represents a tribute in four distinct ways: The foremost homage is paid to the spiritual inspiration and the central ﬁgurehead of SSIOM by taking the middle portion of the sacred name, Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba. He is the source of inspiration, focus and motivation for the young adults in MySAIYa. SAI is also an acronym that stands for " Seek Always Inside ." This acronym emphasises the signiﬁcance of spirituality as the fundamental principle of the wing. This is because many times when at the risk of losing our purpose or reducing to become just another humanitarian organisation, we pray that the name MySAIYa reminds the central purpose (just like how SAI is in the centre of the name) of our existence which is spiritual fulﬁlment as opposed to the outward journey which signiﬁes a tendency to become entangled in

the challenges and setbacks of the world. In this context, "seek" refers to us, the individuals and members of MySAIYa. We are spiritual seekers; in other words, we actively pursue spirituality as a goal rather than engaging in MySaiYa as a recreational platform or leisure hobby. "Always" pertains to the constant, consistent, long-term aspect of this holistic and comprehensive path towards spiritual perfection. We understand Spiritual advancement is not instantaneous; it requires continuous persistent introspection, devotion, selﬂess service, discipline, and personal development. It is a calling to inculcate a spiritual lifestyle and to imbue MySAIYa in every aspect of our work, family and friends with a profound sense of divine connection. Thirdly, SAI emphasises our expected outcomes to contribute in three domains, i.e. Spiritual evolution, Association elevation and Individual transformation . The quest for Spiritual Evolution refers to a transformative process that transcends mere intellectual understanding to become an experiential awakening. It's about connecting with Swami within, the essence of our being, recognising the divinity that permeates every aspect of the universe, and realising our intrinsic unity with all creation. It's a call to elevate our lives from the mundane to the sublime, turning every action, thought, and emotion into a reﬂection of Sathya Sai. Association Elevation speaks to the power of community and the transformative inﬂuence of collective endeavour. MySAIYa believes in the strength of Sangha (spiritual community) to uplift, inspire, and support each individual on their path. This elevation creates an environment where positive values are nurtured, shared, and lived. We elevate ourselves and those around us through mutual encouragement, shared experiences, and the collective pursuit of higher ideals. It's about fostering a culture of kindness, empathy, and understanding

that transcends boundaries, making society a reﬂection of our highest aspirations. Individual Transformation is the personal journey of growth and self-improvement that each member embarks upon. It's about harnessing the potential within to become the best version of oneself. This transformation involves a holistic approach—encompassing mind, body, and spirit. It's about developing a balanced character, honing one's talents and abilities for the greater good, and cultivating a sense of purpose that guides one's actions. Individual transformation is deeply intertwined with selﬂess service, devotion, and the pursuit of wisdom. It's a journey of becoming, where each step forward is a step towards realising one's divine nature and potential to eﬀect positive change in the world. Finally, SAI also elucidates our methodologies to achieve the above, which encompass the tripartite paths of Service (engaging in community-uplifting endeavours, embodying the principles of Karma Yoga - the path of selﬂess actions as a spiritual practice), Adoration (expressing faith in God through devotional practises, exemplifying bhakti Yoga), and Illumination (pursuing wisdom and knowledge as a spiritual discipline, embodying the principles of Jnana Yoga). The aﬃrming Ya Following “My” and “SAI” are the “Ya” alphabets designed to signal how MySAIYa represents the term "Young Adults," which eﬀectively deﬁnes the speciﬁc demographic we serve, thereby ensuring a clear and relevant purpose. The “Ya'' in MySAIYa unequivocally represents "Young Adults," reﬂecting our core demographic.

The other subtler intent for Ya is to be a positive response which means “yes” in the local Bahasa Melayu (Malay) language. Here, it is intended as a positive aﬃrmation of the earlier call to “Seek Always Inside” through our methodology of “Service, Adoration and Illumination”. Deep still, "Ya" is also a colloquial contraction of the word "you" in English and is often used in informal conversations (for example, "How ya doin'?" ). So, at the core, the name MySAIYa integrates to see all unity. It equates My and Ya with the Sai in the middle as the bridge to bring this realisation of oneness. The end goal of SAIYa This is followed by SAIYa, which phonetically represents the Malay language ‘saya’ /Saa-jə/, a pronoun referring to oneself. Here, the play of words intends to point towards the goal of identifying the true nature of the Self or, in other words, Self-realisation symbolically.
