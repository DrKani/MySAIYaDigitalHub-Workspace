MY SAI YES 23-25 ALSO HIGHLIGHTS AN IMPERATIVE SHORT-TERM MISSION SET FOR THE YEAR 2025 Mission 2025: Facilitate, catalyse and enable a vibrant, inclusive, and proactive young adults’ community in SSIOM. To realise this vision, MySAIYa strives to elevate, empower, and engage young adults by inspiring them through meaningful opportunities and inclusive platforms where they feel seen, heard, and inspired to embrace leadership roles. By actively encouraging them to step into leadership positions within the SSIOM national programs, state teams, and spearhead centre activities, we aim to ignite a passion for contributing back to their local Sai centre through programs, events, and

Animal care - Papan Dog Feeding 2 weekly at Ipoh: Papan Dog Feeding is a bi-monthly service activity led by young adults in Ipoh. The aim of this initiative is to provide food for dogs living in the Papan dumpsite. This site is where dogs caught by the Ipoh Municipal Council are kept, with the goal of reducing the number of strays in the city. The project is carried out with the help of
