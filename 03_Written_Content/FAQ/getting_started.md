# Getting Started

## Malaysian participation and account eligibility

### Do I have to change my religion to participate?

No. Sai teachings encourage sincerity to one’s own faith, without conversion. In Malaysia, site membership and account features are limited to non-Muslim Malaysians. Our community services and general non-religious initiatives remain available to everyone; your Centre can advise on spiritual participation.

### Is the Sathya Sai Organisation interfaith?

Yes. Mutual respect, unity and universal Human Values bring together people from different racial, cultural and religious backgrounds. This respect is practised within Malaysian law: site membership and account features are for non-Muslim Malaysians, while community services and general non-religious initiatives remain open to everyone.

### Do I need to know about Sathya Sai Baba before visiting?

You do not need prior knowledge of Sathya Sai Baba. Contact your local Centre to ask about activities and spiritual participation under SSIOM’s Malaysian guidelines. Our non-religious community services are available to everyone.

### I don’t consider myself very religious. Can I still come?

You do not need advanced spiritual knowledge or experience. Your Centre can help you find suitable activities and explain eligibility for spiritual participation in Malaysia. Non-religious community services are available to everyone.
