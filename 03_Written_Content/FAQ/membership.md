# Membership

## Malaysian participation and account eligibility

### Do I have to become a member to participate?

Formal membership is not required for many regular Centre activities, though spiritual participation follows Malaysian law and SSIOM’s guidelines. Your Centre can explain what is suitable. Registration for site membership and full account engagement, including commenting and user profiles, is limited to non-Muslim Malaysians. Community services and general non-religious initiatives remain open to everyone. The Terms of Use explain the access policy.
