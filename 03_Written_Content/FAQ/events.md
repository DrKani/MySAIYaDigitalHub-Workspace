# Events

## Malaysian participation and account eligibility

### Do I have to register to attend?

Register when the event page asks you to. Some regular activities need no registration, but spiritual events still follow Malaysian law and SSIOM’s participation guidelines; check with the organiser if unsure. Non-religious community initiatives remain open to everyone. Creating a site account is limited to non-Muslim Malaysians.

### Can I bring a friend or family member?

Check the event’s participation guidance before inviting someone. Spiritual gatherings follow Malaysian law and SSIOM’s guidelines; our community services and general non-religious initiatives are available to everyone. For events needing registration, follow the organiser’s instructions for guests.
