# Privacy And Account

## Malaysian participation and account eligibility

### Who can create a MySAIYa account?

Account holders must be aged 18 or above and be non-Muslim Malaysians. Site membership and full account engagement — including commenting, user profiles, forums and polls — follow the access policy in our Terms of Use. Our community services and general non-religious initiatives remain available to everyone, regardless of religious beliefs, ethnicity, gender or age.
