One Lotus, Many Paths

The story behind our logo — five human values, five great faiths, and the one religion of love that holds them together.

MySAIYa Editorial ·24 September 2026·8 min read

You have seen it on banners, name tags and the cover of every programme booklet. But our logo is more than a mark of affiliation with the Sri Sathya Sai International Organisation. It is a small map of the whole spiritual journey.

At its centre stands a pillar crowned by a lotus and a flame. Around it bloom five petals. In the original emblem, each petal held the symbol of one of the world's great religions. Today, they carry the five human values every faith shares: Truth, Right Conduct, Peace, Love and Non-violence.

This piece explores both — and why, for a community as diverse as Malaysia's, that shift matters.

03 · The pillar at the centre

The Sarva Dharma Stupa

The pillar in our logo is real. It stands near the temple in Prasanthi Nilayam, Bhagawan's main ashram in Puttaparthi.

Read the pillar from the ground up. Its rising column signifies humanity rising above differences, towards the unfolding of the lotus of the heart.

05 · In Prasanthi Nilayam

A symbol you cannot miss

Today the Sarva Dharma symbol is synonymous with Prasanthi Nilayam. It greets you on the arch into Puttaparthi, repeats across the blue, white, pink and yellow buildings along the road, and rests on the front face of the marble Mahasamadhi in Sai Kulwant Hall.

06 · Malaysian roots, Sai wings

A logo for every Malaysian

In 1997, the five values logo came to represent the international organisation — speaking beyond any single belief and suiting the religious climate of every country. It fitted Malaysia's multilingual, multiethnic fabric naturally. The Majlis Pusat Sathya Sai Baba Malaysia used a trilingual version from 1997 to 2014. Today, SSIOM carries the clean, crisp logo adopted by all SSSIO-affiliated bodies since 2014.
