# Coordinated Experience Strategy

## Core Visitor Journey

**Who Is Bhagawan? → His Life → His Message → His Work → What Will I Do With What I Have Discovered?**

The four pages should form one coherent journey while remaining independently useful.

| Page | Core question | Experience |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction | Who is Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba? | Sacred-modern orientation |
| His Life | What happened across His lifetime and mission? | Illustrated biographical journey |
| His Message | What did He teach and how can it be practised? | Thematic teaching pathways |
| His Work | What happens when spiritual values become action? | Human-needs impact journey |

# Shared Design Language

Use the MySAIYa design system while giving this section a calmer and more contemplative character.

Recommended principles:

- generous whitespace;
- strong editorial typography;
- restrained motion;
- high-quality approved archival imagery;
- dark navy or midnight-indigo content sections;
- temple-gold accents used selectively;
- Sai cyan for interactive navigation and informational elements;
- the MySAIYa hero-stroke gradient only as a subtle connective device;
- no excessive glowing effects;
- no generic spiritual stock photography;
- no invented imagery presented as historical fact.

# Introduction Page

The introduction should orient rather than overwhelm.

Recommended components:

1. Cinematic but restrained hero
2. “How Do We Begin?” opening
3. “Why Bhagawan?” explainer
4. Five Human Values preview
5. Unity of Faiths section
6. Love Made Visible transition
7. Young Adults relevance section
8. Three exploration cards: Life / Message / Work
9. Closing personal invitation

# Life Page

The page should feel like a journey through time rather than a long article.

Recommended components:

1. Hero with approved historical image
2. Early life chapter
3. 1940 declaration milestone
4. Prasanthi Nilayam milestone
5. Organisation growth
6. Education chapter
7. Healthcare chapter
8. Water chapter
9. Worldwide movement
10. Final years
11. “A Life That Continues Through Action” closing

Use a responsive timeline on desktop and stacked milestone cards on mobile.

# Message Page

Avoid a quotation-wall design.

Organise teachings into meaningful pathways:

1. The Divinity Within
2. Five Human Values
3. Love All, Serve All
4. Help Ever, Hurt Never
5. Unity of Thought, Word and Deed
6. Mastery of the Mind
7. Ceiling on Desires
8. Education and Character
9. Work as Worship
10. Devotion
11. Service
12. Unity of Religions
13. Self-Confidence to Self-Realisation
14. Start Where You Are

Each pathway may include:

- concise teaching explanation;
- approved quotation where verified;
- practical application;
- optional reflection prompt;
- save-to-briefcase functionality for registered users.

# Work Page

Do not design the page as a catalogue of achievements.

Organise around human needs:

**To Learn → Education**

**To Heal → Healthcare**

**To Live → Drinking Water**

**To Belong → Community Service**

Then connect these areas through shared principles:

- dignity;
- access;
- excellence;
- selflessness;
- professional competence;
- transformation of the person serving.

# Source and Content Governance

Before public launch:

- verify historical dates against authoritative Sathya Sai sources;
- verify all direct quotations;
- maintain a source register for statistics;
- avoid unsupported superlatives;
- distinguish devotional belief from externally verifiable historical claims;
- use approved photographs with documented provenance and permissions;
- avoid AI-generated representations of historical events unless clearly identified as artistic interpretations.

# Cross-Linking

Introduction → Life, Message, Work

Life → Introduction, Message, Work

Message → Life, Work, MySAIYa Mission

Work → Introduction, Life, Message

# Recommended Future Enhancement

Create a structured `sai-teachings` content collection.

Suggested fields:

- teaching_id
- title
- theme
- summary
- verified_quote
- source_reference
- practical_application
- reflection_prompt
- related_human_value
- related_content
- languages
- publication_status

This would allow the Digital Hub to reuse verified teachings across articles, quizzes, learning modules, reflections, quote cards and the registered user's Briefcase without duplicating or losing source information.
