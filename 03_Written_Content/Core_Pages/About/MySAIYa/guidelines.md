## A Community of Works in Progress

MySAIYa does not operate like a membership club with a scorecard. We understand that every young adult who walks through our doors — literally or digitally — is on a journey. The pace is personal. The path is one's own.

There is no strict monitoring system. What we do have is a clear set of shared commitments — agreed values and practices that help us show up consistently for each other and for the communities we serve.

---

## The 9-Point Code of Conduct

All MySAIYa members are encouraged to align with the 9-point Code of Conduct observed across the global Sathya Sai movement:

1. **Daily meditation and prayer**
2. **Engaging in selfless service** at least once a week
3. **Attending Sai Centre programmes** regularly
4. **Refraining from speaking ill of others** — especially fellow Sai members
5. **Refraining from alcohol, tobacco, and other intoxicants**
6. **Observing vegetarianism** (or progressively working toward it)
7. **Refraining from partisan politics** within the Sai fold
8. **Taking care of one's health** as a responsibility, not a right
9. **Treating all people** — across race, religion, and status — as expressions of the same divine presence

## Interfaith & Inclusive by Design

MySAIYa is not a Hindu organisation. It is an interfaith community. While many of our members come from Hindu, Buddhist, or Taoist backgrounds, and while devotional practices may carry elements of these traditions, our community is open to all — with one caveat: *in compliance with Malaysian law, membership and devotional activities are open for non-Muslim participation*, while we warmly encourage our Muslim friends to engage with their local mosque or Muslim organisations.

We respect and honour the principle of **non-proselytisation** emphasised by Sathya Sai Baba Himself — encouraging every person to deepen their faith in God according to their own religious inclination.

---

## Gender-Considerate Practices

MySAIYa observes gender separation in certain programme settings — a practice in keeping with SSIOM's broader guidelines and reflecting sensitivity to the diverse cultural and religious values of our members. This is not a restriction; it is an expression of thoughtful inclusion.

---

## Political Neutrality

MySAIYa maintains strict political neutrality. The organisation does not endorse, affiliate with, or campaign for any political party or candidate. Our service is to people, not parties.

---

## Dress & Conduct

Members attending MySAIYa events are encouraged to dress modestly and conduct themselves in a manner that reflects the values we hold — with kindness, respect, and awareness of the diverse people who share our spaces.

## MySAIYa Code of Practices (Supplementary)

### Adherence to the 9-Point Code of Conduct, the 10 Guiding Principles, and Legal Compliance

MySAIYa's activities are conducted in strict accordance with all legal statutes governing non-profit organisations in Malaysia, ensuring compliance with national law and relevant international guidelines — including the **Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA)** and the **Copyright Act 1987**.

Beyond legal compliance, members are self-accountable to the shared commitments listed in this document. MySAIYa does not police its members. We trust that those who come to us seeking growth will hold themselves to the standard they have set for themselves.

---

### Participation in Active SSIOM Sai Centre Membership

MySAIYa members are encouraged to be active participants in a local Sai Centre — the foundational unit of SSIOM life. Regular attendance at bhajan sessions, study circles, and service activities is not a requirement but a deeply recommended practice for those who wish to grow within the Sai framework.

---

### Digital Conduct

On all MySAIYa digital platforms — including this website, WhatsApp groups, and social media — members are expected to conduct themselves with the same values they would bring to a physical gathering: with respect, kindness, honesty, and care for others' dignity.
