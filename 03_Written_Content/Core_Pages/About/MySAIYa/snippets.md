# MySAIYa in 60 Seconds

## Hello. We’re MySAIYa.

**Malaysian Sai Young Adults.**

A community of young adults aged 18 to 40 within the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia.

We learn.

We serve.

We create.

We lead.

We reflect.

We sing.

We laugh.

We sometimes have no idea what we are doing.

We learn from it.

And we keep growing.

All while trying to answer one very important question:

**How can we live a more purposeful life?**

Welcome to MySAIYa.

Here is the fast version.

---

Malaysian Sai Young Adults.

Across Malaysia.

From knowing more...

to doing better...

to becoming who we truly are.

## So... What Exactly Is MySAIYa?

Good question.

MySAIYa is the Young Adult Wing of SSIOM.

But that description only tells you where we sit organisationally.

In real life?

MySAIYa is a community.

A platform.

A learning space.

A service network.

A spiritual journey.

A place to discover talents.

A place to make friends.

A place to ask difficult questions.

A place to take responsibility.

A place to make mistakes and grow from them.

And, for many people, a place that gradually begins to feel like family.

## Do I Need to Be “Very Spiritual”?

No.

And we are not entirely sure what “very spiritual” looks like anyway.

You can come with questions.

Curiosity.

Uncertainty.

Enthusiasm.

A desire to serve.

A desire to learn.

Or simply because someone invited you.

Spiritual growth is a journey.

Nobody is expected to arrive at the destination before joining the community.

---

## Is MySAIYa a Separate Organisation?

No.

MySAIYa is an integral part of the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia.

SSIOM provides the organisational, spiritual and governance framework within which MySAIYa operates.

We are one part of a much larger national and international Sai movement.

[Discover SSIOM →](/about/ssiom/overview)

---

## Is MySAIYa a Religion?

No.

MySAIYa is part of SSIOM, an interfaith spiritual organisation inspired by the teachings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba.

We respect all religions.

We uphold non-proselytisation.

We believe that spiritual growth should help people become better practitioners of their own faith and better human beings.

---

## Why 18 to 40?

Because young adulthood is complicated.

Wonderful.

Exciting.

Confusing.

Demanding.

And transformative.

Between 18 and 40, many of us are:

- studying;
- starting careers;
- changing careers;
- building relationships;
- starting families;
- discovering independence;
- accepting responsibilities;
- questioning old assumptions;
- searching for meaning; and
- trying to work out what kind of life we actually want to live.

MySAIYa creates opportunities to explore these years with spiritual purpose and a supportive community.

---

## Do I Have to Pay to Join?

There is no membership fee to become part of the MySAIYa community.

Our activities are organised within SSIOM and made possible largely through volunteers who contribute their time, talents and energy.

Specific events or activities may have their own participation arrangements where necessary.

## What If I Don't Know Anyone?

Many people did not know anyone when they first arrived.

Then someone said hello.

That is often how community begins.

You do not need an existing group of friends to participate.

Come to a programme.

Join an activity.

Volunteer.

Attend a gathering.

## What If I Don't Have Any Special Talents?

Excellent.

Neither did most people when they started.

MySAIYa is not only a place to contribute talents.

It is a place to discover them.

You may join to help with registration and discover you enjoy organising.

Volunteer for media and discover photography.

Join a service project and discover leadership.

Attend a bhajan and discover music.

Help with a digital initiative and discover technology.

Sometimes the ability appears before the opportunity.

Sometimes the opportunity reveals the ability.

Both are welcome here.

## Is Everything Serious and Spiritual?

We are serious about spirituality.

We are not convinced spirituality always needs to look serious.

MySAIYa believes that spiritual growth can happen through:

- friendship;
- creativity;
- music;
- service;
- conversation;
- reflection;
- leadership;
- culture;
- games;
- travel;
- shared experiences; and
- joy.

You can seek meaning and still laugh.

You can practise spirituality and still have fun.

In fact, we highly recommend both.

---

## What Is Selfless Service?

Service is the practice of using our time, abilities and energy for the benefit of others without expecting personal reward.

But there is another side to it.

When we serve sincerely, something changes within us too.

We become more aware.

More grateful.

More compassionate.

Less centred on ourselves.

So while service may begin with helping someone else, it often becomes part of our own transformation.

## What Happens at a Sai Centre?

Sai Centres are local communities within SSIOM.

Activities vary, but may include:

- devotional singing;
- prayer and meditation;
- study circles;
- spiritual discussions;
- service activities;
- festival programmes;
- community initiatives; and
- Young Adult activities.

For many members, the Sai Centre is where the MySAIYa journey becomes part of regular community life.

Volunteers.

Young adults serving at local, regional and national levels.

MySAIYa operates through an interconnected leadership structure within SSIOM.

Different people carry different responsibilities.

But the philosophy is shared:

Leadership is an opportunity to serve.
