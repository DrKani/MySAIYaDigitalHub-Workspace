## Our Mission (In Full)

> **"To empower young adults ages 18 to 40 with sound human values, inspired by the teachings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, so they lead purposeful lives and create positive impact wherever they may be."**

---

## 5 Things You Should Know About MySAIYa

1. **We are not a religion.** MySAIYa is a values-based community, not a religious institution. Members come from Hindu, Buddhist, Taoist, Christian, and other backgrounds. What unites us is not a creed but a commitment.
2. **We are genuinely interfaith.** While we draw inspiration from Sathya Sai Baba's teachings, we deeply respect every faith tradition and actively encourage members to be better practitioners of their own religion.
3. **Our programmes are free.** All MySAIYa activities, programmes, and resources are offered at no cost. Service is its own reward.
4. **We are part of something global.** As the young adult wing of SSIOM — affiliated with the SSSIO — MySAIYa is connected to a network of young adult Sai movements in over 150 countries.
5. **We take fun seriously.** Sathya Sai Baba taught that spirituality should be embraced with joy, not solemnity. MySAIYa creates experiences that are meaningful *and* genuinely enjoyable.

## Common Questions, Quick Answers

**Who can join MySAIYa?**  
Any non-Muslim young adult aged 18 to 40 who resonates with our values and wants to be part of a community committed to growth and service.

**Do I need to be a Sai devotee to join?**  
Not at all. Many members come out of curiosity, or because a friend invited them. Come with an open heart — that's enough.

**Is there a membership fee?**  
No. There are no fees to participate in MySAIYa programmes or community activities.

**What happens when I turn 40?**  
You graduate — with our deepest gratitude — into the broader SSIOM community and carry the MySAIYa spirit forward in whatever capacity suits your life.
