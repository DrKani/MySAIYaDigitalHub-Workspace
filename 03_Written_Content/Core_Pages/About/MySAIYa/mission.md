## Our Vision

> **"To facilitate young adults to realise and manifest their inherent Divine Nature."**

This is not a corporate aspiration. It is a spiritual one — and intentionally so.

We believe that every young adult, regardless of background or belief system, carries within them a nature that is fundamentally good, fundamentally capable, and fundamentally divine. MySAIYa exists to create the conditions in which that nature is recognised, nurtured, and expressed — in relationships, in service, in leadership, in daily life.

---

## Our Mission

> **"To empower young adults ages 18 to 40 with sound human values, inspired by the teachings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, so they lead purposeful lives and create positive impact wherever they may be."**

The mission is put into action through programmes and experiences centred on:
- **Self-introspection** — creating the quiet to hear your own deeper truth
- **Selfless service** — showing up for others without agenda
- **Devotion** — cultivating a sincere and joyful inner relationship with the divine
- **Integration** — carrying what we learn in the Sai Centre into our homes, workplaces, and communities

## Our Values: Nine Guiding Principles

1. **We embody the love of Sai.** Love is not a sentiment — it is a practice. We aspire to lead with genuine, unconditional love in all our interactions.
2. **We belong to Malaysia and serve her.** Our service is for all Malaysians — regardless of race, religion, or background. We are an asset to this nation.
3. **We uphold the five human values.** Truth, Right Conduct, Peace, Love, and Non-Violence are not aspirations on a poster. They are the measure of our daily choices.
4. **We engage in selfless service as our spiritual pledge.** Service without expectation of reward is the highest form of worship. We serve because it is who we are, not because of what we will receive.
5. **We take up Sai leadership as a sacred calling.** Leadership in MySAIYa means being of use — channelling our skills, creativity, and energy toward meaningful impact.
6. **We pursue human excellence.** Our excellence springs not from pride but from humility — the quiet conviction that we owe the world our very best.
7. **We embrace spirituality with fun and joy.** Spirituality should be a source of delight, not a burden. We create spaces where young adults can love, laugh, and grow simultaneously.
8. **We pledge first to be, then to do, and only then to tell.** We draw from personal practice. We commit to daily Namasmarana, dietary discipline, punctuality, kindness, and respect for elders — not as rules, but as choices that keep us anchored.
9. **We are committed to the Sai collective discipline.** We actively engage as SSIOM members: observing gender-considerate practices, wearing modest attire, fostering inclusivity, and engaging in selfless activities.
