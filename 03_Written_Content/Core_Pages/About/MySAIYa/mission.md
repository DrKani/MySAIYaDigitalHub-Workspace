# About MySAIYa

## Where purpose meets joy

What if your spiritual journey didn’t mean stepping away from life — but stepping into it more fully?
That’s the idea at the heart of MySAIYa, the Malaysian Sai Young Adults. We’re a vibrant community of young adults aged 18 to 40, inspired by the timeless teachings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, discovering that service, devotion, and self-discovery aren’t things you do on weekends — they’re a way of living, everywhere you go.

# Our Vision

***To guide young adults in realising and manifesting their inherent Divine Nature by leading purposeful and exemplary lives.***
We believe something simple but radical: every young adult — regardless of background or belief — carries a fundamentally good, capable, and divine nature. Our role is to create the space for that nature to be recognised, nurtured, and expressed — in relationships, in service, in leadership, and in everyday life.

# Our Mission

***To empower young adults with sound human values, inspired by the teachings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, so they lead purposeful lives and create positive impact wherever they may be.***
The keywords? Wherever they may be. The lessons learnt at the Sai Centre aren’t meant to stay there. We carry them home. Into our workplaces. Into our communities. That’s where transformation becomes real.

# What we do

Our approach is simple yet profound — blending selfless service, introspection, and devotion into experiences that grow the whole person:
	•	Nurturing Talent — your energy, expertise, and ideas have a home here
	•	Self-Introspection — the inward journey that powers everything else
	•	Selfless Service — hands that help, for the community and the environment
	•	Deepening Devotion — meditation, devotional singing, and study circles that anchor the heart
	•	Leadership — real opportunities to lead change that matters
	•	Cultural Understanding — celebrating our traditions under one guiding truth: Fatherhood of God, Brotherhood of Man
And yes — we do all of this with fun and joy. Spirituality here isn’t solemn and distant. It’s alive, energetic, and shared with friends who become family.

# Our compass

## Nine guiding principles keep us true to who we are:

These guiding principles are drawn from the precedent set by Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba. They are intended to help MySAIYa members strive to follow the values and ideals He espoused throughout His life. These foundational principles form the backbone of our collective character, aspirations, and actions — they encapsulate who we are, what we do, and why we do it. In essence, they serve as an internal compass, keeping us balanced, focused, and true to our purpose in MySAIYa.
**	1.	**We are spiritual aspirants.
**	2.	**We centre ourselves in Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba.
**	3.	**We are ambassadors of Human Values.
**	4.	**We embrace selfless service as our spiritual pledge.
**	5.	**We take up Sai leadership as a sacred calling.
**	6.	**We pursue human excellence.
**	7.	**We embrace spirituality with fun and joy.
**	8.	**We pledge first to Be, then Do, and only then Tell.
**	9.	**We are committed to the Sai collective discipline.

# Our story

Our journey began in 1997 as the Youth Wing, becoming the National Young Adult Wing in 2004, and today we go by MySAIYa. The name has evolved — the spirit never has. We are the young adult wing of the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM), operating under its constitution and guided by the values Sathya Sai Baba embodied: love, truth, peace, righteousness, and non-violence.
**Come as you are**
Whether you’re seeking deeper meaning, a community that lifts you up, a place to serve, or simply friends walking the same path — there’s a place for you here.
Join us. Discover who you truly are — and watch what you can become.
MySAIYa — realising the divine, one purposeful life at a time.
