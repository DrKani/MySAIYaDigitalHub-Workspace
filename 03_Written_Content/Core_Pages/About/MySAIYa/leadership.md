## About the MySAIYa Committee

The **MySAIYa Committee** is a dedicated group of young adult volunteers — professionals, students, creatives, and community builders — who give their time, skills, and energy to ensure MySAIYa continues to grow as a meaningful, impactful, and joyful community.

We serve not because we were asked to fill positions, but because we believe in what this community is becoming.

---

## Our Commitment as a Team

The MySAIYa Committee aspires, above all, to deepen love at its core. Our work is not administrative — it is relational. We plan programmes, yes. But more importantly, we try to create spaces where people feel genuinely welcomed, genuinely seen, and genuinely inspired.

We are not perfect. We are works in progress, just like every member we serve.
