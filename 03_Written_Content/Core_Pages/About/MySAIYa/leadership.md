# Leadership at MySAIYa

## Leadership as an Opportunity to Serve

At MySAIYa, leadership begins with a simple question.

**How can I serve?**

Not:

What position can I hold?

How much authority will I have?

Or how visible will I become?

The leadership philosophy of MySAIYa is grounded in the teachings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba and the organisational framework of the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM).

We understand leadership as responsibility.

A responsibility to people.

To purpose.

To values.

To the Organisation.

And to the trust placed in us.

---

## We Are Part of SSIOM

MySAIYa is the Young Adult Wing of the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia.

It is not a separate organisation.

Our national, regional and local young adult initiatives operate within the organisational framework, constitution and guidance of SSIOM.

This relationship is fundamental to understanding MySAIYa leadership.

We are part of a larger collective.

Our programmes contribute to a larger mission.

Our leaders work alongside other wings, Sai Centres, regional teams and national office bearers.

And our young adults participate in the broader spiritual and service life of SSIOM.

MySAIYa leadership therefore requires both initiative and collaboration.

The freedom to create.

And the maturity to work together.

---

## The Purpose of MySAIYa Leadership

The purpose of leadership is not merely to organise successful programmes.

Programmes matter.

Planning matters.

Execution matters.

But the deeper responsibility of a MySAIYa leader is to help create conditions in which people can grow.

Where a new young adult feels welcomed.

Where a quiet member discovers confidence.

Where someone with an idea is given an opportunity.

Where talents are recognised and developed.

Where mistakes become opportunities to learn.

Where differences are handled with maturity.

Where service remains selfless.

Where spirituality remains central.

And where the collective purpose remains more important than individual recognition.

---

## From Position to Responsibility

Leadership titles are necessary.

They help define responsibilities.

They create accountability.

They allow an organisation to function effectively.

But a title alone does not make someone a leader.

Within MySAIYa, leadership is expressed through action.

Showing up consistently.

Listening carefully.

Communicating clearly.

Preparing thoroughly.

Keeping commitments.

Treating people respectfully.

Making difficult decisions when necessary.

Accepting feedback.

Acknowledging mistakes.

Sharing credit.

Developing others.

And remembering why the work began in the first place.

Leadership is not measured only by what happens while we are in a role.

It is also measured by what continues after we leave it.

---

# Our Leadership Structure

MySAIYa works through an interconnected leadership network within SSIOM.

Different levels of leadership carry different responsibilities, but all contribute towards a shared national purpose.

---

## National Young Adult Leadership

The National Young Adult leadership helps guide the overall direction, development and coordination of MySAIYa across Malaysia.

Its responsibilities include:

- supporting the vision and mission of SSIOM;
- developing national strategies and priorities for young adults;
- coordinating national programmes and initiatives;
- strengthening communication across regions and Sai Centres;
- creating opportunities for spiritual, service, leadership, cultural and personal development;
- supporting regional and local young adult leaders;
- encouraging collaboration between different parts of SSIOM;
- identifying emerging needs among Malaysian young adults;
- developing sustainable systems, resources and initiatives for future generations; and
- representing the interests and aspirations of young adults within the wider organisational structure.

National leadership is therefore not simply about running national events.

It is about helping the entire Young Adult movement grow.

---

## National Teams and Portfolios

The work of MySAIYa is supported by young adults who contribute through different teams, portfolios, projects and areas of expertise.

Depending on current organisational needs, these may include:

- spiritual development;
- selfless service;
- leadership development;
- communications;
- creative media;
- digital initiatives;
- programmes and events;
- education and learning;
- community engagement;
- member development; and
- special national projects.

These teams allow young adults to contribute according to their abilities and interests while developing new skills through experience.

The objective is not to create unnecessary layers of administration.

It is to create meaningful avenues for participation.

---

## Regional Young Adult Leadership

Malaysia's Sai Centres and communities operate across different regions, each with its own strengths, realities and needs.

Regional Young Adult leaders help connect national direction with local participation.

They strengthen communication between Sai Centres.

Encourage collaboration.

Support local leaders.

Identify challenges and opportunities.

Facilitate regional initiatives.

And help ensure that young adults across different parts of Malaysia remain connected to the wider MySAIYa community.

Regional leadership is particularly important in a national movement where geography should never become a barrier to belonging.

---

## Sai Centre Young Adult Leadership

The Sai Centre is the foundation of organisational life within SSIOM.

For many young adults, their first and most consistent experience of MySAIYa happens locally.

Through devotional programmes.

Service activities.

Study circles.

Centre gatherings.

Friendships.

Mentorship.

And opportunities to participate.

Local Young Adult leaders therefore play an essential role.

They know their communities.

They understand local realities.

They recognise the abilities and needs of individual members.

And they help create the relationships through which a national movement becomes a genuine community.

---

## Leadership Is a Collective Effort

No MySAIYa leader works alone.

Behind every programme are people.

The person who planned.

The person who designed.

The person who called participants.

The person who managed logistics.

The person who arranged food.

The person who solved an unexpected problem.

The person who stayed behind to clean.

The person who quietly checked whether someone was doing alright.

Not everyone needs a title to lead.

Some of the most important acts of leadership happen without one.

MySAIYa therefore seeks to develop a culture where initiative is encouraged, responsibility is shared and contribution is valued.

---

## Young Adults Are Not Merely Leaders of Tomorrow

Young people are often told:

**You are the leaders of tomorrow.**

We believe that statement is incomplete.

Young adults can contribute today.

They can lead projects.

Develop solutions.

Serve communities.

Create new initiatives.

Mentor others.

Build technology.

Produce art.

Teach.

Organise.

Research.

Communicate.

And help shape the future of the Organisation.

Leadership development therefore does not mean asking young adults to wait for responsibility.

It means providing meaningful opportunities to accept responsibility now.

With guidance.

With accountability.

With humility.

And with the freedom to learn.

---

## Leadership as a Spiritual Practice

Leadership reveals us to ourselves.

It shows us how we respond when plans fail.

How we behave when people disagree.

How we react when our ideas are rejected.

Whether we seek recognition.

Whether we listen.

Whether we can apologise.

Whether we can work with people different from ourselves.

Whether we remain calm when responsibility becomes difficult.

For this reason, leadership within MySAIYa can become a powerful form of spiritual practice.

The project may be external.

But the lessons are often internal.

Patience.

Humility.

Discipline.

Courage.

Compassion.

Detachment.

Perseverance.

And love.

---

## Be. Do. Tell.

One of the principles that guides our understanding of leadership is simple:

**First Be.**

**Then Do.**

**Only then Tell.**

Character before communication.

Practice before proclamation.

Example before instruction.

We do not always succeed.

Leadership is itself a journey of learning.

But this remains the standard towards which we strive.

## Leadership Has a Time Limit. Service Does Not.

Organisational roles change.

Committees change.

Responsibilities change.

New leaders emerge.

That is healthy.

MySAIYa does not belong to any individual, committee or generation.

Each leadership team receives something built by those who came before.

Each has a responsibility to care for it.

Improve it.

Strengthen it.

And eventually entrust it to others.

Our responsibility is therefore not to make ourselves indispensable.

It is to help build a movement that can continue without us.

---

## Your Opportunity to Lead

Leadership at MySAIYa can begin anywhere.

Volunteer for a project.

Share an idea.

Help organise an activity.

Offer a skill.

Support someone who is struggling.

Take responsibility for something small.

Do it well.

Learn from the experience.

Then take the next step.

You do not need to know everything before you begin.

You need sincerity.

Willingness.

Responsibility.

And the courage to grow.

**Because leadership in MySAIYa is not ultimately about how many people follow us.**

**It is about how faithfully we learn to serve.**
