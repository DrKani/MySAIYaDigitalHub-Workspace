# The Name MySAIYa

## What's in a Name?

Sometimes, a name simply tells you what something is.

**MySAIYa tells a story.**

It speaks about where we come from.

Who inspires us.

The community to which we belong.

The journey we are taking.

And the person we are trying to become.

**MySAIYa** is the identity of the Malaysian Sai Young Adults.

But within those eight letters are several ideas that express the heart of our movement.

**My.**

**SAI.**

**YA.**

And, together,

**MySAIYa.**

---

# MY

## Malaysian at Heart

The first meaning of **My** is our home.

**Malaysia.**

MySAIYa is a distinctly Malaysian young adult movement.

We are shaped by the country in which we live.

A country of different cultures.

Languages.

Traditions.

Communities.

And faiths.

Our Malaysian identity influences the way we gather, communicate, celebrate, serve and understand one another.

We believe that our diversity is not an obstacle to unity.

It is an opportunity to practise it.

Being Malaysian is therefore not simply a geographical description within our name.

It is part of our responsibility.

To contribute positively to our communities.

To strengthen understanding.

To serve those in need.

To promote human values.

And to participate meaningfully in building a compassionate and harmonious society.

---

## A Personal Journey

**My** also means something deeply personal.

My journey.

My responsibility.

My choices.

My transformation.

MySAIYa may be a community, but spiritual growth cannot be outsourced to a community.

No organisation can practise patience for us.

No committee can perform self-reflection on our behalf.

No programme can transform us without our participation.

The journey must eventually become personal.

What am I learning?

How am I changing?

How do I respond when life becomes difficult?

How do I treat people when no one is watching?

What values guide my decisions?

What am I doing with the talents, opportunities and time entrusted to me?

The **My** in MySAIYa reminds us that transformation begins with the individual.

Before changing the world around us, we must be willing to examine the world within us.

---

# SAI

## Our Spiritual Centre

At the heart of MySAIYa is **SAI**.

Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba.

His life and teachings provide the spiritual foundation upon which MySAIYa stands.

He taught that the fundamental purpose of human life is to recognise our inherent divine nature and express it through character, love and service.

He reminded us that spirituality is not confined to places of worship.

It must enter our thoughts.

Our words.

Our actions.

Our relationships.

Our professions.

Our homes.

And our responsibilities towards society.

To place **SAI** at the centre of our name is therefore to place this aspiration at the centre of our journey.

---

## Seek Always Inside

Within MySAIYa, **SAI** also carries a reminder:

**Seek Always Inside.**

The modern world constantly invites us to look outward.

For approval.

For achievement.

For recognition.

For comparison.

For answers.

The spiritual journey asks us to also look within.

To examine our motives.

Understand our reactions.

Recognise our strengths.

Confront our weaknesses.

Question our assumptions.

And gradually discover the deeper Self beneath the identities we accumulate.

This inward journey is not an escape from the world.

It prepares us to participate in the world more consciously.

The more clearly we understand ourselves, the more meaningfully we can serve others.

---

## Service. Adoration. Illumination.

**SAI** also expresses three interconnected dimensions of the MySAIYa journey.

### Service

The hands.

Using our abilities, energy and opportunities for the benefit of others.

Service teaches us to move beyond self-interest.

It brings us into contact with lives different from our own.

It transforms compassion from an idea into action.

### Adoration

The heart.

Deepening our relationship with the Divine through devotion, prayer, meditation, devotional singing, reflection and remembrance.

Adoration gives spiritual life warmth, humility and love.

### Illumination

The mind.

Growing in understanding through introspection, study, inquiry and the application of spiritual wisdom.

Illumination invites us not merely to collect knowledge, but to transform understanding into character.

**Hands that serve.**

**Hearts that love.**

**Minds that seek truth.**

Together, Service, Adoration and Illumination express the holistic spiritual development towards which MySAIYa aspires.

---

# YA

## Young Adults

**YA** represents who we are.

**Young Adults.**

MySAIYa serves young adults aged 18 to 40 within the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia.

Young adulthood is a remarkable stage of life.

We make decisions that may shape decades.

We pursue education.

Build careers.

Form relationships.

Start families.

Discover abilities.

Experience failures.

Question assumptions.

Search for meaning.

And gradually determine the kind of people we wish to become.

MySAIYa exists to support young adults through this journey.

Not by providing every answer.

But by creating opportunities to learn.

To serve.

To lead.

To reflect.

To develop talents.

To build meaningful friendships.

And to explore how spiritual values can guide the realities of contemporary life.

---

## A Call to Participate

There is also something energetic about **Ya**.

It sounds like an affirmation.

A response.

A willingness to step forward.

To participate rather than merely observe.

To contribute rather than only consume.

To ask:

**How can I help?**

**What can I learn?**

**What can I offer?**

**Where can I serve?**

MySAIYa is built through participation.

Every programme.

Every project.

Every idea.

Every act of service.

Every contribution.

Every young adult who chooses to show up adds something to our collective journey.

---

# SAIYA

## The Journey Towards the Self

When **SAI** and **YA** come together, another meaning emerges.

**SAIYa.**

It echoes the Bahasa Melayu word **saya**.

**I.**

**Me.**

**The self.**

And within that simple linguistic connection lies perhaps the deepest aspiration of MySAIYa.

Who am I?

Not merely:

What is my profession?

What have I achieved?

Where do I come from?

What do other people think of me?

But beneath all these changing identities:

**Who am I, truly?**

The teachings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba continually direct the spiritual aspirant towards this question.

The journey of selfless service.

The practice of devotion.

The discipline of introspection.

The development of character.

The pursuit of human excellence.

All ultimately point inward.

Towards Self-realisation.

Towards recognising the divine nature inherent within every human being.

---

## More Than Branding

MySAIYa is not simply a shorter way of saying Malaysian Sai Young Adults.

It is an identity we grow into.

A reminder that spirituality must become personal.

That selfless service must become practical.

That devotion must deepen the heart.

That knowledge must illuminate life.

That young adults have the capacity to contribute now.

That being Malaysian gives us both an identity and a responsibility.

And that the longest journey we may ever undertake is the journey towards understanding ourselves.

---

## A Name We Carry Together

Every person encounters MySAIYa differently.

For some, it begins with friendship.

For others, service.

A bhajan.

A camp.

A conversation.

A leadership opportunity.

A moment of reflection.

Or simply curiosity.

But wherever the journey begins, the invitation is the same.

**Make it yours.**

Bring your questions.

Bring your talents.

Bring your ideas.

Bring your uncertainties.

Bring your willingness to learn.

Bring your willingness to serve.

And discover what happens when a personal journey becomes part of a shared purpose.

**MY. SAI. YA.**

**Malaysian in identity.**

**Sai at the centre.**

**Young Adults in action.**

**The Self as our destination.**
