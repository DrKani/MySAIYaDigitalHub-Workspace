## Breaking It Down

MySAIYa — three syllables, four layers of meaning.

---

### My — Personal Ownership

The *My* in MySAIYa is not possessive in the selfish sense. It is a declaration of belonging and personal stake. This is *my* community, *my* journey, *my* responsibility. When a young adult says "I am MySAIYa," they are not reading a label — they are making a commitment.

---

### SAI — Spiritual Anchor

*Sai* refers directly to **Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba** — the spiritual teacher whose life and teachings inspire everything we do. It is the thread that connects us to a lineage of unconditional love, selfless service, and the relentless pursuit of truth. Keeping *Sai* at the centre of our name ensures we never lose sight of what we are truly here for.

---

### Ya / SAIYa — Malaysia and the Self

*Ya* grounds us in Malaysia — our home, our context, our community of responsibility. But *SAIYa* goes deeper still. Phonetically, it echoes the Malay word *saya* — meaning "I" or "myself." This wordplay is intentional: the ultimate goal of the MySAIYa journey is **self-realisation** — understanding who the *I* truly is, beneath all the roles, labels, and identities we accumulate.

---

## MySAIYa: A Reflection of Ourselves, Our Nation, and Our Aspiration

In this one name, we find:
- A personal invitation (*My*)
- A spiritual home (*SAI*)
- A national identity (*Ya / Malaysia*)
- An existential destination (*SAIYa / saya / self*)

As we move forward — seeking, serving, soaring — we carry this name not as a brand, but as a promise. To ourselves. To each other. To the world we are here to serve.
