# MySAIYa Coordinated About Experience

This folder contains the revised content for five complementary pages within the MySAIYa About section.

The existing `mission.md` remains the canonical page for MySAIYa's vision, mission, approach and guiding principles.

## Page Roles

| Page | Primary role | Experience pattern |
|---|---|---|
| `mission.md` | What MySAIYa stands for | Purpose-led editorial page |
| `story.md` | Where MySAIYa came from | Editorial timeline |
| `name.md` | What the name means | Interactive typographic explainer |
| `leadership.md` | How MySAIYa serves and organises | Editorial content plus directory |
| `guidelines.md` | How members walk together | Principles accordion |
| `snippets.md` | Fast orientation for newcomers | Interactive quick-start |

## Recommended Navigation Order

1. MySAIYa in 60 Seconds
2. Vision, Mission & Values
3. Our Story
4. The Name MySAIYa
5. Leadership at MySAIYa
6. Our Shared Commitments

## Shared Page System

All six About pages should use:

- the same breadcrumb pattern;
- the same eyebrow typography;
- a consistent hero height;
- consistent section spacing;
- a shared related-pages component;
- one primary and one secondary call to action;
- accessible heading hierarchy;
- readable line length of approximately 65–75 characters;
- anchor links for long pages; and
- subtle use of the MySAIYa hero gradient as a top accent.

## Distinct Visual Treatment

### Story

Use a vertical timeline with milestone years, archival imagery and selected community quotations. Avoid turning the page into a corporate chronology.

### Name

Reveal `MY`, `SAI`, `YA` and `SAIYA` progressively. Use animation sparingly and provide a reduced-motion alternative.

### Leadership

Use an SSIOM relationship diagram followed by national, regional and centre-level cards. The dynamic leadership directory should be driven by structured data rather than hard-coded into the page.

### Guidelines

Use accessible accordion sections. Keep all content available in the HTML for search engines and screen readers. Provide direct links to legal policies.

### Snippets

Use fast-moving fact cards, tap-to-reveal questions and strong pathway cards. This should be the most playful page in the About section without becoming informal or gimmicky.

## Cross-Linking Rules

Each page should link to no more than three contextually related About pages near the end.

Recommended links:

- `snippets` → mission, story, programmes
- `mission` → story, name, get involved
- `story` → mission, name, leadership
- `name` → story, mission, snippets
- `leadership` → volunteer, guidelines, contact
- `guidelines` → leadership and legal policies

## Content Governance

- `mission.md` remains the source of truth for official vision and mission wording.
- `story.md` should only contain dates and milestones that can be verified.
- Leadership profiles should be maintained separately as structured data.
- Legal obligations should remain in legal pages; `guidelines.md` should link to them rather than duplicate them.
- Translations should be completed only after the English master copy is approved.
