## 1. Design Direction

MySAIYa Digital Hub should feel **sacred, modern, warm, trustworthy, and gamified without becoming childish**. Visually, the identity centres on:

- Deep navy anchors.
- Vibrant purple folds and dividers.
- White / off-white backgrounds.
- Elegant, luxurious and rounded, bento-style components, grids, content cards, carousels, slides, cue cards etc.
- Radiant, metallic feel, exquisite gradient category block heading folds.
- Shiny, metallic, hyper-real gold accents.
- A clean, Poppins-led interface.
- Elegant Playfair Display font semi-bold headings in metallic 3D regal gold texture.

The interface should communicate:

- **Devotion:** deep navy, gold, calm spacing, spiritually uplifting typography.
- **Elegance, modernity and clarity:** simple cards, readable text, clear CTAs.
- **Vibrant and youthful:** colourful, radiant and metallic gradient galleries and content cards.
- **Modern spirituality:** inspiring gradients, rounded cards, subtle elevation, friendly microcopy.
- **Uplifting and motivating progress:** customised and personalised dashboards, streaks, levels, badges, milestones, guided journeys.
- **Community:** reflecting SSIOM branding, Malaysian in cultural nuance language and tone, emphasising fellowships and shared offerings and exciting upcoming events.

## 2. Visual Principles

### 2.1 Culturally Rooted and Spiritually Sacred — Never Old-Fashioned

Deep navy, vibrant purple, and metallic gold gradients are paired with generous white space, relaxed spacing, modern card layouts, soft shadows, and clean iconography. Together they signal a spiritual foundation that is deeply rooted yet unmistakably contemporary — elegant and professional rather than ceremonial. Avoid ornate frames, clichéd spiritual motifs, amateur visual treatments, heavy textures, or anything resembling a "temple brochure." Sacredness is communicated through restraint and craftsmanship, not decoration.

### 2.2 Professional yet Joyful

The default experience is organised, calm, and breathable — closer to a polished, premium product than a busy community noticeboard. This composure never comes at the cost of being a youth-centred platform: vibrant, high-energy gradients are deployed deliberately in gallery views, content card headers, feature categories, progress moments (streaks, badges, level-ups), game areas, and achievement states. Spirituality should be a source of joy rather than a burden; devotion, service, and reflection can be approached with genuine lightness without losing their seriousness of purpose.

### 2.3 White Space Is Part of the Brand

Generous spacing around cards and sections is non-negotiable:

- Avoid cramped layouts.
- Avoid long, dense text blocks.
- Avoid screens with more than two primary CTAs visible at once.

### 2.4 Rounded, Gentle, Human

- Most components use large border radii (rounded cards, chips, banners).
- Sharp rectangles are reserved only for full-width structural bars (e.g. nav, status bars).
- No harsh outlines — rely on soft shadows and subtle borders.

### 2.5 Every Page Shows the Journey

Fellowship is at the heart of the Sai community, and the platform should quietly extend that same spirit online — encouraging members to feel part of something collective without ever feeling tracked or pressured. Where possible, show:

- Week number, level, streak, target.
- Reading progress, participation, and reflection history.
- Next milestone badge or "upcoming unlock" moments.
- Peer testimonials and past event involvement.

Progress indicators exist to gently invite continued participation and remind members they're walking this path alongside others — never to gamify devotion into a performance metric.

### 2.6 Visual Hierarchy Principles

1. Meaning before decoration.
2. Content before effects.
3. Action before information.
4. Spiritual message before gamification.
5. One primary CTA per viewport.
6. One visual focal point per section.
7. Avoid competing colours competing for attention.

## 3. Brand Personality

MySAIYa's personality is multifaceted traits held in deliberate balance, drawn directly from the inspirations laid by Sathya Sai Baba while being rooted to the nuanced Malaysian culture resonant to the young adults in SSIOM, so the platform never tips into "preachy NGO" on one side or "generic youth app" on the other. Every blog post, CTA, badge, and microcopy line should be checkable against these pillars and the MySAIYa Guiding Principles and Codes of Conduct.

### 3.1 Sincere and Real

Blog posts, testimonials, opinion pieces, and write-ups carry honesty, genuineness, and down-to-earth values that build a real emotional connection with Malaysian young adults aged 18–40. Post-event reports, programme descriptors, and activity briefs report social impact transparently. First **be**, then **do**, and only then **tell**: action and integrity come before publicity.

- **Voice cue:** First-person stories over institutional press-release tone. Real names, real numbers, real outcomes.
- **Visual expression:** Genuine, lightly-edited photography of real members and real moments over stock imagery or AI-generated visuals. Where photography isn't available, illustration is preferred over synthetic or staged-looking imagery.

### 3.2 Exciting and Alive

Calls to action, event announcements, campaign launches, and programme updates carry spirited, youthful energy — fun, joyful, adventurous, and attention-grabbing — deliberately pushing against the perception that spirituality, philanthropy, culture, religion, morality, and charitable work are boring or rigid.

- **Voice cue:** Verbs over nouns, momentum over formality. "Join the crew this Saturday" beats "Your participation is encouraged."
- **Visual expression:** High-energy gradients in gallery views, content card headers, and feature categories; expressive illustration and iconography for progress moments (streaks, badges, level-ups), game areas, and achievement states; Malaysian cultural motifs (batik patterns, local colour palettes, recognisable landmarks) woven in as accents, not backdrops.

### 3.3 Competent and Professional

A strong, capable, reliable brand identity built on intelligence, success, and efficiency — never rooted in pompousness or superiority. True excellence is humble, shown through action rather than claimed through self-promotion. The platform should feel capable enough to earn trust from parents, committees, and partner organisations, without ever feeling cold or corporate.

- **Voice cue:** Precise dates, clear logistics, no broken links or half-finished forms. Competence is shown, not claimed.
- **Visual expression:** Clear visual hierarchy, consistent card systems, predictable navigation, and responsive interactive components that behave reliably across devices.

### 3.4 Fellowship-Driven and Spiritually Grounded

MySAIYa belongs to every Malaysian Sai young adult, regardless of race, language, or background — bound not by uniformity but by shared human values and a common devotion to Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba's teachings of universal love and "Unity is Divinity." Sincerity gives the brand integrity, excitement gives it energy, competence gives it credibility, and fellowship gives it soul.

- **Voice cues:** Opening with "Om Sai Ram"; dedications to the Lotus Feet of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba at the start or close of formal pieces; warm kinship address throughout — "uncle"/"aunty" for elders, "Bro"/"Sis" before first names for fellow young adults aged 18–40, and affectionate terms for younger members; an inclusive, welcoming tone in every greeting.
- **Visual expression:** Deep navy and gold as the spiritual anchor, with calm, unhurried spacing. SSIOM emblem, Malaysian traditional design elements, imagery of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, multifaith emblems, and architectural motifs drawn from Prasanthi Nilayam used as grounding visual touchstones. Real-person photography follows MySAIYa's dress code and favours gender-separated group shots.

### 4.3 Signature Gold Gradient

`linear-gradient(90deg, #D2AC47 5%, #F7EF8A 45%, #AE8625 85%)` — a dynamic metallic gold gradient, horizontal orientation, used for some portion of texts, background colour for buttons, outlines, underlines etc.

Treat it as a **prestige element**: only for special CTA buttons, achievement cards, milestone banners (not everyday buttons), achievement highlights, badges, premium accents, key icons, occasionally decorative separators. Use sparingly to guide user attention to the most critical actions and branding moments.

- `#D2AC47` (5%): medium, warm gold — primary base on the left.
- `#F7EF8A` (45%): bright, pale-yellow highlight — the sharpest "gleam" just before centre.
- `#AE8625` (85%): rich, deep gold — shadow effect on the right side.

Rules:

- The most common use is for the "SAI" in "MySAIYa". Surrounding letters must remain high-contrast, flat colours (Anchor Navy `#002E5B` on light backgrounds; `#FFFFFF` or `#E0E0E0` on dark backgrounds). Do not mix gradients.
- Always apply a bold/heavy font weight (600+) — Playfair Display, Poppins or Open Sans — to gold letters. Thin fonts bleed the gradient colours together.
- Button text on gold gradient uses Anchor Navy `#002E5B` or `36083D`  **Never white text over a gold gradient.**
- Interaction states mimic how real gold reflects light:

```css
/* Default */
background: linear-gradient(90deg, #D2AC47 5%, #F7EF8A 45%, #AE8625 85%);
background-size: 100% 100%;
transition: background-position 0.4s ease;

/* Hover — the "gleam" effect */
background-size: 200% 100%;
background-position: right center;
box-shadow: 0 4px 15px rgba(210, 172, 71, 0.4);

/* Active / clicked */
transform: translateY(1px);
filter: brightness(0.9);
box-shadow: 0 2px 5px rgba(210, 172, 71, 0.2);
```

- Text links: do not use the full gradient for body links (text gradients break over multiple lines). Use solid gold `#D2AC47` for links, underline on hover.
- Selected/active elements (e.g. selected navigation tab): use a solid 3px bottom border or a left-side vertical accent bar using the gradient.

**Dark-mode variant (High Contrast Gold Signature):** on dark backgrounds (Navy `#002E5B` and its shades; pops best on ultra-dark `#121214` / `#0A0A0C`):

```css
linear-gradient(90deg, #E6C667 0%, #FFF9A6 40%, #855E13 100%)
```

- `#E6C667`: lighter, more vibrant golden-yellow left edge.
- `#FFF9A6`: near white-gold luminous centre reflection.
- `#855E13`: rich bronze-chocolate "inner shadow" making elements look extruded.

### 4.6 Readability Rule for Gradients

- Always test contrast at the brightest point under white text or darkest point under dark text.
- Aim for WCAG AA contrast (at least 4.5:1 for normal text).
- To preserve the gradient and stabilise the text, when in doubt add a subtle overlay:

```css
.level-card, .quote-card { position: relative; overflow: hidden; }
.level-card::before, .quote-card::before {
  content: "";
  position: absolute;
  inset: 0;
  background: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.18);
  pointer-events: none;
}
```

## 5. Component Colour Matrix

### 5.1 Button System

The everyday **primary button must use Navy-900 with white text**. Gold and Gold Gradient must be reserved for special calls-to-action, achievements, offerings, completion moments, and sacred milestone interactions. **Do not use Gold or Gold Gradient as the default primary button style.** Gold must remain meaningful and rare.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Navy-900 | White | None | Scale 1.02; background transforms to Energy Purple `#7B2482`; text becomes bold | Normal main action: Save, Continue, Register, Submit |
| Secondary | White | Navy-900 | None | Background transforms to Energy Purple `#7B2482`; text transforms to white | Alternative action |
| Tertiary | Purple-600 / Purple-700 | White | None | Scale 1.02; 2px gold-gradient border appears; background transforms to Navy-700; text bolds | Interactive or high-energy action |
| Special CTA | `linear-gradient(90deg, #D2AC47 5%, #F7EF8A 45%, #AE8625 85%)` | Navy-900 bold | None | Shift the gradient position (background-size 200%) so the bright centre highlight sweeps across the button | Offering, achievement, milestone, certificate, completion |
| Ghost | Transparent | Navy-900 | None | A border (Navy-100) appears | Low-emphasis action |
| Destructive | `#EF4444` | White | None | Darken 10% | Delete, logout, revoke, cancel irreversible action |

### 5.6 UX & Motion — Mandatory Standards

Motion must clarify feedback, not decorate. Smooth, youthful, purposeful, calm.

| Motion Context | Colour Rule | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hover surface | `--bg-interactive-hover`, `--purple-100`, or `--bg-interactive-muted-hover` | No strong gradients for simple hover |
| Pressed state | Darken base colour, reduce shadow | Avoid dramatic scaling |
| Focus state | `--focus-ring-blue` with `--focus-ring-offset` | Accessibility, not decoration |
| Loading | Skeleton base `--gray-100`, white shimmer | Skeletons for page/card loading |
| Success motion | Success Teal + gentle icon animation | Avoid excessive confetti |
| Achievement motion | Gold or Gold Gradient | Milestone moments only |
| Danger motion | Destructive Magenta | No playful animation |
| Ticker motion | Purple/Navy/updates gradient | Pause on hover/focus; respect reduced motion |

## 6. Theme-Locked Ink Zone Adaptations

## 7. Accessibility — Formal Statement

All interactive elements must meet WCAG AA minimum:

- Text: 4.5:1 (body), 3:1 (large text).
- Icons/graphics: 3:1.

Specific rules:

1. Brand Gold (`#D2AC47`) is **prohibited as body text on light backgrounds**.
2. On light surfaces, Gold is limited to borders, icons, indicators.
3. Focus states: all interactive elements must have visible focus; minimum 2px outline; high contrast.

## 8. Layout System

The layout must feel spacious, calm, structured, and mobile-first: professional yet youthful, with generous breathing room, rounded surfaces, clear information hierarchy. **4px base unit with an 8px primary rhythm.**

Core rules:

1. 4px base unit for fine alignment; 8px increments for most spacing decisions.
2. Main content should not stretch too wide on desktop.
3. Cards and bento layouts must remain spacious and readable.
4. Body text must never run full-width across large screens.
5. Mobile layouts favour stacked content and thumb-friendly spacing.

### 8.1 Base Grid

| Area | Specification |
|---|---|
| Base unit | 4px |
| Primary spacing rhythm | 8px |
| Main content max width | 1280px |
| Standard desktop content width | 1200px |
| Wide dashboard max width | 1400px |
| Legal / reading max width | 1100px |
| Long-form text max width | 720–800px |
| Desktop horizontal padding | 32–40px |
| Tablet horizontal padding | 24px |
| Mobile horizontal padding | 16px |

Use 1280px only for broad content sections and dashboards. Long-form reading, legal pages, reflections and teachings use narrower widths.

## 9. Typography System

Typography must communicate reverence without rigidity, energy without noise, clarity without coldness. Three modes: **sacred display**, **action interface**, **long-form reading**.

### 9.1 Core Typeface Trio (locked)

| Font Family | Usage | Classification | Brand Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| Playfair Display | Hero titles, quotes, sacred emphasis | Serif | Spiritual authority, timelessness |
| Poppins | Interface, navigation, buttons, labels, badges, dashboards | Geometric Sans | Youth, action, modernity |
| Open Sans | Long-form body, legal, accessibility-heavy reading | Humanist Sans | Readability, trust, neutrality |

- **Poppins is the default interface font.**
- Open Sans when reading comfort is more important than interface personality.
- Playfair Display sparingly for headings, sacred, editorial and hero moments.
- No additional fonts without a typography audit. Decorative, handwritten and script fonts are prohibited.

Font loading: `font-display: swap`; always define system fallbacks; avoid loading unused weights; test Tamil and Chinese fallback rendering; no webfont-only styling for critical text.

### 9.2 Type Scale

| Token | Desktop | Mobile | Line Height | Weight | Font | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| display-xl | 72px | 44–48px | 1.05–1.1 | 700 | Playfair Display | Rare landing hero |
| display-lg | 60px | 40px | 1.1 | 700 | Playfair Display | Hero greeting |
| h1 | 48px | 32–36px | 1.15–1.2 | 700 | Playfair Display / Poppins | Page title |
| h2 | 36px | 28px | 1.2–1.25 | 700 | Poppins | Major section title |
| h3 | 28px | 22–24px | 1.25–1.3 | 600–700 | Poppins | Card/module title |
| h4 | 22px | 18–20px | 1.3–1.35 | 600 | Poppins | Sub-section title |
| body-lg | 18px | 16px | 1.65 | 400–500 | Open Sans / Poppins | Hero descriptions, long reading |
| body-md | 16px | 15–16px | 1.55–1.65 | 400–500 | Poppins / Open Sans | Standard UI text |
| body-sm | 14px | 13–14px | 1.5 | 400–500 | Poppins | Card descriptions, hints |
| caption | 12px | 12px | 1.4 | 500–600 | Poppins | Metadata, helper text |
| micro | 10–11px | 10–11px | 1.2 | 700–800 | Poppins | Badges, tickers, tiny labels |

Never jump more than one hierarchy level (avoid H1 → H3 without a bridging H2) unless the layout intentionally creates hero-to-body contrast.

### 9.3 Typography Roles

Sacred display (Playfair 700); quote text (Playfair Italic 500–600); page title (Playfair or Poppins 700); UI heading (Poppins 600–700); body (Poppins/Open Sans 400–500); long-form body (Open Sans 400); label (Poppins 600–800); microcopy (Poppins 500–700); action text (Poppins 700).

### 9.4 Capitalisation Rules

- **Title Case:** sparingly — H1 hero headlines, major campaign banners, formal report covers.
- **Sentence case (default):** H2/H3, blog titles, card titles, body, form labels, alerts, empty states.
- **ALL CAPS:** only short buttons, badges, ticker labels, metadata labels ≤3 words. Always increase letter-spacing (`.text-action { text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 0.08em; }`). Never all-caps paragraphs or long mobile menu labels.
- Never: Title Case in body paragraphs; random mixed casing; more than two type families in one component.

### 9.5 Typographic Voice by Context

- **Divine message (Sai Baba quotes):** Playfair Display Italic, centred, max 60ch, letter-spacing −0.01em; attribution in Open Sans/Poppins sentence case — Gold only on dark backgrounds, Navy on light. Quotes must feel contemplative, not promotional.
- **Action hub (buttons/nav):** Poppins 600–700, tracking 0.06–0.12em, short verb-led labels ("Register now", "Explore", "Get involved", "Continue learning"). Uppercase only for major CTAs; sentence case for secondary/utility buttons.
- **Learning & reflection:** Open Sans preferred; sentence case; paragraph spacing 24–32px; line-height 1.6–1.75; max width 720–800px. Calm conversation, not a lecture.
- **Legal ledger (Privacy/PDPA/Terms):** Open Sans 400; headings Poppins/Open Sans 600–700; neutral text colours only; emphasis via heading structure, not colour; max 1100px container with 720–800px text blocks.

### 9.6 Contrast Rules

| Combination | Status |
|---|---|
| Navy on Warm White | Allowed |
| Black-soft on Warm White | Preferred for body |
| White on Navy / Purple | Allowed |
| Gold text on light background | **Prohibited** (icons/borders only) |
| Gold text on dark background | Allowed, short accents only |
| Purple text on white | Allowed (links, labels, emphasis) |
| Gray-400 on white | Avoid for body; non-critical metadata only |

User-controlled text scaling: Standard 16px, Large 18px, Extra 20px. Scaling must not break hierarchy.

### 9.7 Emphasis & Mechanics

Prefer font weight, clear structure, short paragraphs, callout boxes. Italics only for Sai Baba quotes, Sanskrit/Tamil terms, titles of works, short contemplative emphasis. Avoid excessive exclamation marks, full-bold paragraphs, random colour emphasis. Lists: consistent end punctuation; avoid long bullet lists inside cards; numbered steps for workflows.

## 17. Component Library v1.1

Six rules that decide everything else:

- **Callouts:** one per screen. Emblem watermark: 7% opacity, bleeding off one corner, never centred behind text, only on navy/gradient surfaces. Playfair numeral watermarks in gray-100 stay under 1.3:1 contrast. Certificate PDFs carry the mono lockup at 12% opacity bottom-right with the SSIOM emblem at equal weight.

### 17.3 Feedback & States

- **Toasts:** bottom-right desktop, bottom-centre mobile; 4s auto-dismiss, max 2 stacked, pause on hover; enter with 220ms rise + fade; `role="status"`. Navy-900 with white text — they float over any surface, never a light background. One optional gold action, one dismiss. Achievement toasts are the only ones allowed the purple-metallic gradient, and only for real milestones.
- **Tooltips:** navy-900, white text, 12px radius, `--shadow-lg`; label tooltips 1–3 words; rich tooltips max 280px / 3 lines / one link. Never contain a form control, never the only path to an action or essential info. 150ms delay in, instant out; flip at viewport edges; dismiss on Escape/scroll/blur; touch gets tap-to-open popover; wire `aria-describedby`.
- **Skeletons** for known layouts (mirror real geometry exactly; text lines vary in width, last line short); spinners only for actions under 1s. Determinate bars are navy; the gold ring is reserved for full-page loads. Never show a spinner and a skeleton together.
- **Empty/error/success states:** one human sentence, one next step, never blame. Empty states describe the future, not the member's inaction ("Your briefcase is empty… they will be waiting" — not "You haven't saved anything yet"). Errors use `role="alert"`; no error codes in headlines.
- **Modals:** max 480px wide (640px for forms), 48px radius, scrim `rgba(6,22,47,0.62)`, one primary action, focus trapped (moves to heading on open, returns to trigger on close, Escape closes). Destructive confirmations swap the gold button for red and name the consequence in the title.
- **Notification centre:** filter tabs (All/Events/Journey), unread markers, relative timestamps, "Mark all read", "View all notifications".

### 17.4 Forms & Data

- Fields 48px tall, 12px radius, one column by default. Required = red asterisk in the label; optional says "optional" in plain words. Persistent labels; helper text under the field.
- Multi-step registration: three short steps, numbered progress, one primary action per step, always bottom-right; Back is a ghost button.
- **Tables:** header row gray-100 with uppercase 11px labels; 1px gray-100 row rules, never zebra striping; numbers right-aligned in tabular figures; row hover navy-100 wash. On mobile, tables become stacked cards — never sideways scrolling.
- **Charts:** navy for the main series; accents only when a category needs its own meaning. Donuts: four slices maximum, label every slice (a legend alone fails colour-blind readers). Trend: single navy line, soft navy area fill, gold dot on the latest point only. Every number honest — no decorative statistics.
- **Flowcharts:** equal-width navy-100 blocks, Lucide glyph, two-word title, one line of detail; only the outcome step carries gold; arrows gray-300, never curved or dashed.
- **The ten levels:** Aspirant 0 pts → Seeker 400 → Sevak 1,000 → Sadhaka 2,200 → Karma Yogi 5,000 (…continues to Level 10). Infographics on navy carry the level gradients; on white they carry navy blocks only. Never place ten gradients on one light screen.

### 17.5 Page Furniture

- **Heroes — three kinds only:** photo hero (45–65% navy overlay, 4px gradient stroke at the very top, Title Case H1 in Playfair, one gold CTA + one ghost CTA; never text over an un-overlaid photo) · navy hero (section landings without a photograph; emblem watermark at 7%) · member greeting ("Om Sai Ram, {name}" in Playfair, date above, progress chips below; **never a marketing CTA here**).
- **Page headers:** shorter than a hero — breadcrumb, title, one line of context, optional action. Blog headers: category + date + read time, title, standfirst, author row.
- **Email header:** 640px table width, navy-900 band, emblem at 36px (never the full lockup at email sizes); gold is a flat 4px rule, not gradient text (clients drop `background-clip`); body in Open Sans with system fallbacks — no web fonts assumed; one CTA button 44px tall (navy or gold) with a text-link fallback beneath.
- **Footer:** navy-950 base, four link columns, the approved subtitle line, both emblems and the devotional sign-off. SSIOM emblem is attribution only and never sits larger than the MySAIYa lockup. Legal links are gray and never colour-highlighted.
- **Icon containers:** tinted square for categories, gold circle for achievements, ghost square for utilities. Sizes 16 / 20 / 24 / 32.

## 20. Game & Quiz UI Additions (v4 — extends §13)

- **Gallery / card-grid pattern:** 3-up desktop, 2-up tablet, 1-up mobile; 16–24px gap; never more than 3 cards per row. Header fold = category gradient block, 40px top radius, icon chip + eyebrow label + Playfair title; white body with one-line description. One gradient per category. Hover: lift 4px, shadow to Level 2, header gradient unchanged.
- **Level colour** cycles by level number (see relocked `--gradient-level-1…10`) and never signals difficulty by colour alone — the label carries "Level N".
- **Grid boards** (word search / crossword): navy fill, monospace or tabular-figure letters, white text, active cell highlighted gold.
- **Quote-builder tiles:** outline chips, tap to move into the dashed drop zone; purple gradient for the primary "Check" action only.
- **HUD bar:** timer = navy fill; score = red outline (the only red use outside error states); pause = solid navy circle, always top-right.
- Correct/incorrect feedback uses the semantic success/error tokens — never colour alone.
