## A Network Built on Love

Across the length of Peninsular Malaysia and beyond, **54 active Sathya Sai Baba Centres** serve as the grassroots units of SSIOM — local gathering points where members come together for bhajans, study circles, service planning, children's classes, and community fellowship.

They are humble, by design. You will not find grand architecture or elaborate facilities. What you will find is a group of people — diverse in background, united in purpose — who show up every week to practice what they believe: that a life of love and service is not an aspiration but a daily choice.

---

## What Happens at a Sai Centre

Regular activities at Sai Centres include:
- **Weekly bhajan sessions** — devotional singing for eligible participants
- **Study circles** — structured discussion of spiritual texts and teachings
- **Sai Spiritual Education (SSE)** — weekly character development classes for children aged 6–17
- **Service projects** — medical camps, food distribution, environmental drives
- **Celebrations** — marking Sai calendar events, national festivals, and community milestones

---

## A Global Thread, a Local Presence

SSIOM's 54 centres are part of a worldwide network of over **1,200 Sai Centres** in more than **126 countries**. Each centre, regardless of geography, shares the same fundamental purpose: to help people of all faiths and backgrounds find a path to more meaningful, value-driven lives.

## Malaysian membership and participation

Site membership and account features are limited to non-Muslim Malaysians. Our community services, societal commitments and general non-religious initiatives remain available to everyone, regardless of religious beliefs, ethnicity, gender or age. Contact your Centre for guidance on spiritual participation under Malaysian law and SSIOM’s guidelines.
