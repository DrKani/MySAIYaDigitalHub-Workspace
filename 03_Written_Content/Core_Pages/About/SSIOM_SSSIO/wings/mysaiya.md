A community of Malaysian Sai Young Adults, aged 18–40, growing through friendship, spiritual practice and selfless service. MySAIYa is the Young Adult Wing of the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM), administered under its constitution, rather than a separate organisation.

Inspired by Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, we connect inner growth with everyday action. We learn at Sai Centres, serve in our communities, create music and cultural experiences, and help one another take on responsibility.

## Meaning of the name

- **MY:** Malaysian identity and personal ownership of the journey.
- **SAI:** Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba as our inspiration; Seek Always Inside.
- **YA:** Young Adults and the Malay *ya*, meaning yes — an affirmation of the spiritual path.
- **SAIYa:** Echoes the Malay *saya*, meaning I, and invites inquiry into the Self.

The original emblem expresses this identity through Bhagawan’s silhouette, the youthful handwritten *ya*, a circular community beyond borders, blue depth of purpose and orange passion for action. Show the original artwork without recolouring it; distinguish this emblem from any newer brand assets.

## From Within, Into the World

Service (Karma Yoga) puts compassion into action through our hands. Adoration (Bhakti Yoga) nurtures devotion through the heart. Illumination (Jnana Yoga) develops understanding through study, reflection and inquiry. These complementary paths form SAI.

## Vision and mission

**Vision:** To facilitate young adults to realise and manifest their inherent Divine Nature.

**Mission:** To empower young adults ages 18 to 40 with sound human values, inspired by the teachings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, so they lead purposeful lives and create positive impact wherever they may be.

The five human values are Truth (Sathya), Right Conduct (Dharma), Peace (Shanti), Love (Prema) and Non-violence (Ahimsa).

## Principles and practice

The nine principles: embody Sai’s love; belong to and serve Malaysia; uphold the five human values; embrace selfless service; take up Sai leadership; pursue human excellence; embrace spirituality with fun and joy; First Be, then Do, then Tell; uphold Sai collective discipline.

The public code summary retains dignity, respect for every faith without conversion, service without reward, caring speech, responsible digital boundaries, and accountable leadership. It is an introductory summary, not a replacement for SSIOM’s complete guidelines.
