## Governance Overview

SSIOM operates through a clear, structured governance model that ensures accountability, continuity, and the faithful pursuit of its mission.

---

## The Central Council

The highest governing body is the **SSIOM Central Council**, comprising 18 office bearers:
- President
- Deputy President
- National Secretary
- National Treasurer
- 4 Vice Presidents (Service, Spiritual, Education, SCA)
- 2 Assistant Secretaries
- 2 Assistant Treasurers
- 6 Council Members (Sathya Sai Academy, Ladies Wing, Young Adult Wing, and 3 Advising Members)

---

## The Executive Committee (EXCO)

For routine and urgent decision-making, the Central Council appoints an **Executive Committee (EXCO)** comprising the President, Deputy President, Vice Presidents, National Secretary, and Treasurer — empowered to act on behalf of the Central Council between formal meetings.

---

## Sathya Sai Baba Foundation of Malaysia

A **company limited by guarantee** (Registration: 201101010934), incorporated on **April 5, 2011**, the Foundation handles all legal and asset-related matters — managing properties and donated funds on behalf of SSIOM. It is overseen by a board of **9 trustees** appointed by the Central Council.

---

## Sathya Sai Academy for Human Values Berhad

Incorporated on **May 16, 2013** (Registration: 201301016445 / 1046278-X), the Academy serves as SSIOM's focal point for educational and self-development programmes based on human values. Its separate legal status ensures that SSIOM's multicultural, multireligious educational offerings maintain full credibility and independence.

---

## The Wings

SSIOM's programmes are delivered through distinct wings, each serving a specific community need:

| Wing | Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Spiritual Wing** | Devotional practices, faith deepening, inner transformation |
| **Service Wing** | Community service, welfare, and social upliftment |
| **Education Wing** | Sai Spiritual Education (SSE) for children aged 6–17 |
| **Sai Chinese Affairs (SCA) Wing** | Chinese community engagement in Mandarin |
| **Ladies Wing** | Women's empowerment and spiritual development |
| **Teen Youth Wing** | Transition programme for ages 15–17 |
| **MySAIYa (Young Adult Wing)** | Young adults aged 18–40 |

---

## Sai Centres: The Grassroots

At the most local level, **54 active Sai Centres** across Malaysia function as the basic operational units of SSIOM — each a community hub where members gather for bhajans, study circles, service planning, and fellowship.
