## Who We Are

The **Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM)** is a non-profit, non-governmental organisation established in Malaysia — a distinguished beacon of hope, transformation, and community upliftment, rooted in the life and teachings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba.

Our membership is multi-ethnic, multi-religious, and drawn from every walk of Malaysian life. This diversity is not incidental — it is the point. SSIOM has always believed that love, when genuine, does not recognise the boundaries that history has drawn between us.

---

## Our Core Commitment

SSIOM exists to improve lives — irrespective of race, religion, ethnicity, culture, language, or socioeconomic background.

Over four decades, we have become synonymous with:
- **Human values-centred living** — Truth, Right Conduct, Peace, Love, Non-Violence
- **Selfless service** — free programmes, medical camps, educational outreach, and community care
- **Interfaith harmony** — celebrating what unites us far more than what divides us
- **Youth empowerment** — investing in the next generation as stewards of a better Malaysia

---

## Our Guiding Inspiration

SSIOM draws its energy and direction from **Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba** — one of the twentieth century's most compelling spiritual figures. His teachings on unconditional love, selfless service, and the discovery of one's inherent divine nature provide both the roadmap and the motivation for everything SSIOM does.

He taught us that serving another human being is the highest form of worship. SSIOM takes that seriously.

## A Quick Fact Worth Knowing

The name *SSIOM* only came into official use in **2014**. When the organisation was first registered with the Registrar of Societies Malaysia on **October 8, 1983**, it bore the name *Majlis Pusat Sathya Sai Baba Malaysia* (Sathya Sai Baba Central Council of Malaysia). On **March 4, 2014**, it was formally renamed to align with its global parent, the Sri Sathya Sai International Organisation — joining over 150 countries under a unified identity.
