    How to play?

    1. Roll the dice and move your token.
    2. Every tile is an adventure. Do what it says!
    3. Pass a challenge to earn Karma points. Miss it and step back 2 tiles.
    4. Gold 🎡 tiles spin the Bonus Wheel.
    5. Grace ⬆ lifts you up. Lesson ⬇ slides you back.
    6. First to tile 100 wins!
