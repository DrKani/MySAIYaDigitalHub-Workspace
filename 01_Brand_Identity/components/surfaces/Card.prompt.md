The workhorse container. White by default; switch to `dark` or `shiny` for spiritual surfaces, add a category `accent` border for the card matrix.

```jsx
<Card hoverable>Standard white card</Card>
<Card variant="shiny">Divine message — navy + gold border</Card>
<Card glass accent="service">Service card with teal top border</Card>
```

`variant`: `default` / `dark` (navy-800) / `shiny` (navy + gold gradient border). `accent`: leadership (purple), service (teal), spiritual (violet), education (gold), multimedia (cyan), or any CSS colour. `glass` frosts a white card; `hoverable` lifts it 4px. Radius is the large `--radius-2xl`.
