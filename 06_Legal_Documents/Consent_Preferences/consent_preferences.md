Welcome to MySAIYa's Digital Hub. This document outlines the categories of user data consent and marketing communication preferences managed on the MySAIYa Digital Hub's Privacy Dashboard.

---

## Consent Categories

### 1. Essential Cookies (Required)
* **Description:** Necessary for the website to function, such as maintaining your login session and security tokens. These cannot be disabled.

### 2. Website Analytics (Optional)
* **Description:** Helps us improve our platform by collecting anonymous usage statistics, tracking page load speeds, and identifying technical errors.

### 3. Personalised Recommendations (Optional)
* **Description:** Suggests spiritual quotes, articles, and youth events based on your reading and interaction history on the Hub.

### 4. Newsletter Emails (Optional)
* **Description:** Allows us to email you our monthly digital digest, spiritual insights, and updates from the MySAIYa editorial team.

### 5. Event Announcements (Optional)
* **Description:** Sends notifications and invitations for upcoming regional youth events, study circles, and National Sai Young Adults meetups.

### 6. Volunteer Opportunities (Optional)
* **Description:** Alerts you to upcoming community service (seva) projects, soup kitchens, blood donation drives, and disaster relief programs.

### 7. Surveys & Feedback (Optional)
* **Description:** Enables invitations to participate in site feedback forms, program evaluations, and platform improvements.

### 8. Youth Research Participation (Optional)
* **Description:** Includes you in research, needs assessments, and demographics surveys looking at spiritual practices and challenges among Malaysian youth.

### 9. Event Media Usage (Optional)
* **Description:** Confirms your consent to display photographs or recordings of you taken during community service and public events on our site.

### 10. Public Leaderboards (Optional)
* **Description:** Shows your first name, center region, and points on public quizzes, interactive challenges, or community seva leaderboards.

### 11. Public Member Profile (Optional)
* **Description:** Allows other authenticated MySAIYa members to find your profile, view your shared spiritual stories, and send messages.

### 12. Homepage Showcase Stories (Optional)
* **Description:** Permits our editorial team to publish your submitted reflections, testimonials, or service stories directly on the website homepage.

## Updating Preferences
Users may modify preferences at any time. When logged in, your settings can be managed by navigating to:  
**Account** &rarr; **Privacy & Consent**

---

## Accountability & Consent Records
To ensure complete compliance and audit integrity under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA Malaysia), MySAIYa securely logs:
* The specific consent type (e.g. newsletter, analytics)
* Date of submission / toggle change
* The version of the privacy policy active at the time (e.g. v1.0)
* The withdrawal timestamp when toggled off
