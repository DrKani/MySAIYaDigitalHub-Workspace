# Accessibility Statement

**Effective Date:** 14 January 2026

Om Sai Ram.  
Our loving salutations at the Divine Lotus Feet of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba.

Welcome to MySAIYa's Digital Hub, an initiative by the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM) dedicated to disseminating love, wisdom, and spirituality inspired by the life, teachings, and works of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba. MySAIYa exists to support and empower Malaysian young adults (ages 18–40) on their spiritual, cultural, and service-oriented journey.

This site, the MySAIYa Digital Hub (“the Site”) is an online platform operated by the Malaysian Sai Young Adults (MySAIYa) under the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM), a nonprofit organisation registered with the Registrar of Societies Malaysia (ROS) since 1983 (PPM-001-14-10081983). As part of SSIOM, the platform strictly adheres to a non-solicitation, non-proselytisation, and universal human values approach, ensuring that all content is inclusive and accessible to people of all backgrounds. The MySAIYa Digital Hub is committed to ensuring that every visitor, regardless of ability, can access our content, participate in our community, and benefit from our educational and spiritual resources. Accessibility is an expression of our belief that every individual deserves dignity, respect, and equal opportunity to learn, serve, and grow.

---

## Our Commitment

We strive to make the Digital Hub accessible to users with:
* visual impairments
* hearing impairments
* motor disabilities
* cognitive disabilities
* temporary impairments
* older devices and slower internet connections

---
