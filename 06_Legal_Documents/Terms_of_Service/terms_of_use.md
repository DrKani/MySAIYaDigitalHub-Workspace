## 1. Introduction
The MySAIYa Digital Hub ("the Site") is an online platform operated by the Malaysian Sai Young Adults (MySAIYa) under the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM).

### Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM)
The Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM) is a Malaysian non-profit organisation listed under the Societies Act 1966, with licence number PPM-001-14-10081983, and has been established and registered with the Registrar of Societies Malaysia (ROS) since October 1983. As a multifaceted spiritual movement with service as its core practice, SSIOM provides the platform for its multicultural, multi-religious and multiracial members to put into practice the inspiring teachings of one of the world's revered spiritual teachers, Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba through its vibrant mosaic of social welfare, humanitarian, traditional, devotional, youth development, philosophical, community improvements, educational outreach, cultural nourishment, and nation-building endeavours aimed for elevating the collective consciousness of the society.

### Malaysian Sai Young Adults (MySAIYa)
The Malaysian Sai Young Adults (MySAIYa) is a vibrant movement of volunteers aged 18 to 40 within the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM). United by the universal teachings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, we are a diverse community from many races, faiths, and cultural backgrounds committed to cultivating human values, uplifting society, strengthening national unity, and serving the poor, sick, and needy. Guided by the MySAIYa Committee under SSIOM's constitution, we strive to live with purpose, serve with love, and inspire a new generation of Malaysians to rise in human excellence—transcending all boundaries of religion, ethnicity, gender, and age.
