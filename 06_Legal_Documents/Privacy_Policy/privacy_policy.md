# MySAIYa Digital Hub — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 23rd November 2026

## 1. Introduction
The MySAIYa Digital Hub ("the Site") is an online platform operated by the Malaysian Sai Young Adults (MySAIYa) under the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM).

### Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM)
The Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM) is a Malaysian non-profit organisation listed under the Societies Act 1966, with licence number PPM-001-14-10081983, and has been established and registered with the Registrar of Societies Malaysia (ROS) since October 1983. As a multifaceted spiritual movement with service as its core practice, SSIOM provides the platform for its multicultural, multi-religious and multiracial members to put into practice the inspiring teachings of one of the world's revered spiritual teachers, Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba through its vibrant mosaic of social welfare, humanitarian, traditional, devotional, youth development, philosophical, community improvements, educational outreach, cultural nourishment, and nation-building endeavours aimed for elevating the collective consciousness of the society.

### Malaysian Sai Young Adults (MySAIYa)
The Malaysian Sai Young Adults (MySAIYa) is a vibrant movement of volunteers aged 18 to 40 within the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM). United by the universal teachings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, we are a diverse community from many races, faiths, and cultural backgrounds committed to cultivating human values, uplifting society, strengthening national unity, and serving the poor, sick, and needy. Guided by the MySAIYa Committee under SSIOM's constitution, we strive to live with purpose, serve with love, and inspire a new generation of Malaysians to rise in human excellence—transcending all boundaries of religion, ethnicity, gender, and age.

### ACCESS RESTRICTION (MALAYSIAN LAW)
In light of Article 11(4) of the Federal Constitution of Malaysia and applicable state laws on the propagation of non-Islamic religions among Muslims, our platform’s access policy limits registration for site membership and full engagement (including commenting and user profiles) exclusively to non-Muslim Malaysians. Relevant state provisions include, where applicable, sections 5 and 6 of Selangor’s Non-Islamic Religions (Control of Propagation Amongst Muslims) Enactment 1988.

We respect all faiths. Muslim brothers and sisters interested in joining a spiritual community are respectfully encouraged to participate in activities within Islamic organisations and institutions across Malaysia.

Our community services, societal commitments and general non-religious initiatives remain available to all individuals, regardless of religious beliefs, ethnicity, gender or age. This membership restriction does not apply to receiving these services.

Given the multicultural context we serve, MySAIYa is committed to protecting your privacy, safeguarding your personal information, and complying with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA Malaysia) alongside global best practices. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you interact with the MySAIYa Digital Hub.

## 2. Information We Collect
We may collect the following categories of data depending on your level of interaction:

### a. Personal Information (Provided by You)
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Age range and gender (where voluntarily submitted)
- Sai Centre affiliation (optional)
- Profile details (bio, avatar, interests)
- Quiz participation, event registration, submissions, or feedback
- Photos, videos, or testimonials you voluntarily upload
- Account login information (for Google/Facebook login)

### b. Device & Usage Data (Automatically Collected)
- IP address
- Browser type, operating system, device type
- Language settings
- Access time and date
- Pages visited and duration
- Clickstream data
- Cookies and analytics identifiers

---

## 3. How We Collect Your Data
Your data may be collected through:
- Account creation (Google/Facebook login)
- Website forms (feedback, submissions, blog contributions)
- Event registration forms on the Hub
- Interactive features (quizzes, polls, reports)
- Cookies and analytics tools such as Google Analytics
- Voluntary uploads such as stories, photos, reflections, or articles

No biometric or sensitive personal data is collected.

---

## 4. Purpose of Data Collection
We collect and process your information to:
- Provide you with personalised platform features
- Manage user profiles and dashboards
- Track quiz scores, badges, achievements, and contributions
- Enable commenting, voting, and other interactive features
- Support event sign-ups and volunteer participation
- Improve website functionality and user experience
- Send important updates, newsletters, and announcements
- Detect fraud, secure accounts, and maintain site integrity
- Generate anonymised insights to enhance SSIOM and MySAIYa programs

We do not use your data for commercial purposes.

---

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing
MySAIYa processes data based on:
- Your consent (e.g., creating an account, submitting content)
- Legitimate interests (e.g., improving platform services, maintaining security)
- Performance of a service (e.g., providing interactive features)
- Compliance with Malaysian law where required

You may withdraw your consent at any time (see Section 11).

## 6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies
The Site uses cookies to:

- Government authorities only when legally required

All partners handling data are bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations.

## 8. International Data Transfers
Our Site may use hosting or digital tools with servers located outside Malaysia. Where this occurs, MySAIYa ensures that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information.

## 9. Your Rights Under PDPA
You have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information
- Withdraw consent for optional data usage
- Request deletion (where not restricted by legal/operational needs)
- Request information on how your data is processed
- Lodge a complaint with Malaysian data protection authorities

To exercise these rights, email info@ssiomya.com.

## 10. Data Security
We implement technical and organisational security measures including:
- Secure hosting with firewalls
- SSL-encrypted forms
- Role-based access to administrative areas
- Routine backups and integrity checks
- Account authentication via Google/Facebook

Despite our best efforts, no digital system is completely secure. Please exercise caution when sharing sensitive data online.

For photographs or videos from MySAIYa events:
- Group photos, service activities, and event media may be used for organisational reports or digital documentation.
