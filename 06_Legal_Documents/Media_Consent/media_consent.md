# Media Consent Policy

**Effective Date:** 14 January 2026

Om Sai Ram.  
Our loving salutations at the Divine Lotus Feet of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba.

Welcome to MySAIYa's Digital Hub, an initiative by the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM) dedicated to disseminating love, wisdom, and spirituality inspired by the life, teachings, and works of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba. MySAIYa exists to support and empower Malaysian young adults (ages 18–40) on their spiritual, cultural, and service-oriented journey.

---

## 1. Introduction
At the MySAIYa Digital Hub, we celebrate the vibrant spiritual and service journeys of our community. As a hub active in hosting events, sharing photo galleries, highlighting member spotlights, and publishing video and livestream content, media forms an essential part of how we document, share, and inspire others with the message of love and selfless service inspired by Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba.

---

## 2. Purpose of Media Collection
We collect and publish media primarily to share the spirit of service and community. This media may be utilised across various platforms and publications, including:
* The MySAIYa Digital Hub website
* Annual reports and organisational updates
* Community newsletters and digital mailings
* Promotional posters and event flyers
* Official social media channels
* Public livestream archives
* Educational presentations and workshops

---

## 3. Types of Media Collected
Media covered under this policy includes, but is not limited to:
* **Photos:** Digital and print photographs of event participants, volunteers, and activities.
* **Videos:** Event highlights, session recordings, and short-form video features.
* **Audio:** Sound recordings from study circles, musical programs, and interviews.
* **Livestreams:** Real-time broadcasts of major events, webinars, and public functions.
* **Testimonials & Quotes:** Shared reflections, feedback, and statements from participants.
* **Interviews:** Recorded or written conversations discussing community service or personal growth.
* **Creative Submissions:** Artwork, writings, or multimedia projects submitted by members.

---

## 4. Your Consent
Participation in MySAIYa events, programs, and activities may result in the capture of photographs or digital recordings. We handle consent as follows:
* **General Event Media:** Attending public events or service activities implies that you may be captured in general crowd photos or videos. Group photographs taken during public events may be published to document the activity.
* **Individual Consent:** For close-up portraits, featured interviews, member spotlights, or personal testimonials, explicit written or verbal consent will be obtained where practical.

---

## 5. Withdrawal of Consent
We respect your right to privacy. If you see a photo, video, or quote of yourself published on our digital platforms that you wish to have removed, you may request its removal.

Upon receiving your request, we will make reasonable efforts to delete or blur the media from our active website pages and social media feeds. However, please note that physical printed publications, pre-existing offline media, or historical archives may not always be amendable.

---

## 6. Children & Minors
Protecting the privacy of children is of paramount importance. For any media featuring children or individuals under the age of 18 (e.g. Bal Vikas or SSE events), explicit consent from a parent or legal guardian is strictly required before any close-up photos, videos, or identifying details are published.
