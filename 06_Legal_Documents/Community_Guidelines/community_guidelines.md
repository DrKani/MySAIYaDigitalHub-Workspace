# MySAIYa Digital Hub — Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** 23rd November 2026

---

## Introduction

The MySAIYa Digital Hub ("the Site") is an online platform operated by the Malaysian Sai Young Adults (MySAIYa) under the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM).

These guiding principles are drawn entirely from the precedent set by Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba and form the foundation of our collective character. They guide our aspirations and actions and encapsulate who we are, what we do, and why we do it.

They are intended to aid the members of MySAIYa in striving to act according to the values and ideals espoused by Him throughout His life. In other words, it is a form of internal compass that helps us stay balanced and focused and make decisions aligned with our purpose in MySAIYa.

Henceforth, it is imperative to acknowledge that any endeavours, undertakings, ventures, and gatherings that resonate with these fundamental principles are in perfect harmony with our ultimate purpose. Similarly, any involvement that strays from these principles runs the risk of being superficial and tokenistic and may lead to a detachment of MySAIYa from the original vision and mission of the Sathya Sai movement.

---

### Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM)
The Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM) is a Malaysian non-profit organisation listed under the Societies Act 1966, with licence number PPM-001-14-10081983, and has been established and registered with the Registrar of Societies Malaysia (ROS) since October 1983. As a multifaceted spiritual movement with service as its core practice, SSIOM provides the platform for its multicultural, multi-religious and multiracial members to put into practice the inspiring teachings of one of the world's revered spiritual teachers, Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba through its vibrant mosaic of social welfare, humanitarian, traditional, devotional, youth development, philosophical, community improvements, educational outreach, cultural nourishment, and nation-building endeavours aimed for elevating the collective consciousness of the society.

### Malaysian Sai Young Adults (MySAIYa)
The Malaysian Sai Young Adults (MySAIYa) is a vibrant movement of volunteers aged 18 to 40 within the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM). United by the universal teachings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, we are a diverse community from many races, faiths, and cultural backgrounds committed to cultivating human values, uplifting society, strengthening national unity, and serving the poor, sick, and needy. Guided by the MySAIYa Committee under SSIOM's constitution, we strive to live with purpose, serve with love, and inspire a new generation of Malaysians to rise in human excellence—transcending all boundaries of religion, ethnicity, gender, and age.

### ACCESS RESTRICTION (MALAYSIAN LAW)
Please note that, in compliance with Article 11(4) of the Federal Constitution of Malaysia and relevant state enactments (such as Sections 5 and 6 of the Non-Islamic Religions (Control of Propagation Among Muslims) Enactment), registration to site membership and full engagement (e.g., commenting, user profiles) on this platform are limited exclusively to non-Muslim Malaysians. Muslim brothers and sisters interested in joining a spiritual community are respectfully encouraged to participate in activities within Islamic organisations and institutions across Malaysia. However, our community services, societal commitments, and general non-religious initiatives are available to all individuals, regardless of their religious beliefs, ethnicity, gender, or age.

## Part A: MySAIYa Guiding Principles

### Principle 1: We are spiritual aspirants
We understand that spirituality is the foundation upon which all our endeavours rest in MySAIYa. Notably, our meetings, programmes, activities, and initiatives prioritise spirituality. This is our distinction with the countless community social welfare nonprofits and religious bodies. The societal goals we strive for in our initiatives anchor in the enriching soil of personal spiritual growth and transformation.

This spirituality we speak about isn't just an abstract concept; it's the bedrock of our existence. It is not merely confined to intellectual understanding but is meant to be lived, manifested and experienced in every aspect of life. By infusing every aspect of our work with a deep sense of spiritual connection, we attempt to create an environment where we, along with the young adults entrusted to us, embark on a transformative journey of self-discovery and inner growth.

### Principle 2: We centre our core in Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba
Cultivating a strong affinity to Sathya Sai Baba is essential as we represent His teachings through our conduct in the organisation. For some of us, He is the Divinity Himself meanwhile, for many, He is an enigma, and His extraordinary philanthropy, magnetic leadership, and divine love continue to inspire us. Whatever the relationship, affinity to Sathya Sai Baba means acknowledging His pure love within us and imminent in everything around us and striving to realise that Love experientially and manifest that Love in our thoughts, words and actions.

This is what we collectively consider as faith in Sathya Sai Baba, and this faith isn't a mere sentiment; it's an integral part of who we are. Faith is not characterised by unfounded belief or mindless compliance in this context. Instead, it pertains to the deliberate effort to apply His enduring wisdom in our lives, thereby incorporating love, compassion, and selfless service into our daily actions. As a means to develop this faith, we see Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba as our admirable role model and put His universal teachings and exemplary life of selfless dedication to humanity at the centre of everything we do, from alignment on our long-term vision and mission to our minute day to day operational decisions.

### Principle 3: We are ambassadors of Interfaith Unity and Human Values
At MySAIYa, we recognize the profound importance of promoting interfaith unity and embodying human values in all our endeavors. This principle is not merely a guiding philosophy but a practical commitment that we live by, ensuring that our actions and interactions reflect the universal teachings of Sathya Sai Baba and the core values of humanity.

#### Embracing Interfaith Unity
- **Respect for All Religions:** We acknowledge and respect the diversity of religious beliefs and practices. By fostering an environment where different faiths are celebrated and understood, we create a platform for mutual respect and learning.
- **Inclusive Programs and Activities:** Our programs and activities are designed to be inclusive, welcoming individuals from all faith backgrounds. This inclusivity helps bridge gaps and build stronger, more cohesive communities.
- **Interfaith Dialogues and Events:** We organize interfaith dialogues and events that encourage open discussion and sharing of spiritual insights. These events serve as a medium to promote understanding and harmony among various religious groups.

#### Embodying Human Values
- **Love:** Central to our interactions is the expression of pure, unconditional love. We strive to see the divine in everyone and treat each individual with the utmost care and compassion.
- **Truth:** Honesty and integrity are the cornerstones of our conduct. We are committed to being truthful in our thoughts, words, and actions, reflecting the authenticity of our spiritual journey.
- **Peace:** We promote inner and outer peace through our activities. By cultivating a peaceful mind and environment, we contribute to the overall harmony and well-being of our communities.
- **Right Conduct:** Ethical behavior and moral integrity guide our decisions and actions. We uphold righteousness in every aspect of our work, ensuring that our conduct aligns with the highest standards of ethics and morality.
- **Non-violence:** Our commitment to non-violence extends beyond physical actions to include words and thoughts. We advocate for peaceful resolutions to conflicts and encourage a compassionate approach in all our dealings.

#### Practical Implementation
- **Workshops and Seminars:** Conducting workshops and seminars focused on interfaith unity and human values. These sessions provide practical tools and insights for living harmoniously in a diverse society.
- **Community Service:** Engaging in community service projects that reflect our commitment to human values. These projects address the needs of the underprivileged and promote social equity and justice.
- **Educational Programs:** Developing educational programs that teach the importance of interfaith unity and human values to young adults. These programs help instill a sense of global citizenship and moral responsibility.
- **Collaborations:** Partnering with other organizations and religious groups to promote interfaith harmony and human values. Collaborative efforts amplify our impact and foster a spirit of collective responsibility.

By adhering to these principles, MySAIYa aims to be a beacon of unity and a living example of the transformative power of human values. Our commitment to interfaith unity and the embodiment of human values not only strengthens our spiritual foundation but also contributes to the creation of a more compassionate, inclusive, and peaceful world.

### Principle 4: We engage in selfless service as our spiritual pledge
Prioritising the needs of others, nurturing empathy and compassion, and awakening social responsibility are not mere charitable actions - they are expressions of our earnest effort and profound dedication to our spiritual journey as Sai Young Adults.

We know that the act of service is an indispensable prerequisite laid by Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba for every Sai spiritual seeker. For us, service is not simply an ethical obligation but a sacred pathway that propels us towards personal fulfilment and spiritual enlightenment. Beyond humanistic or altruistic reasons, we strive to meet pressing needs in our communities with empathy and compassion to encourage our personal spiritual growth.

An essential element required to elevate our practice of kindness to spiritual practice is the need for sacrifice. Therefore, a Sai young adult performing selfless service does not necessarily equate to volunteers, benefactors, philanthropists or good samaritans.

It is understood that the expression of selfless service occurs organically when one is internally driven by spirituality. However, we also recognise that the inverse is also valid. This approach catalyses our individual spiritual development as our inclination towards spirituality deepens, fostering a sense of selflessness that becomes ingrained in our character. Just like daily prayers and reflections deepen our self-awareness, selfless actions resonating with some sacrifice elements help us move beyond selfish motivations and self-importance. By balancing inner transformation and outer service, we walk the path of spirituality in action. As we grow more inclined in spirituality, this selflessness becomes part of our being.

Simultaneously, through evidential experience, we know that engaging in acts of service allows us to develop essential skills such as leadership, teamwork, and problem-solving, contributing significantly to our personal growth and development. Besides that, in society, it yields several positive outcomes, including the alleviation of suffering, the spread of well-being, and the expansion of joy.

However, as aspirants strive for spirituality, the higher goals of spiritual fulfilment take precedence over these social objectives. The necessary attitude of selflessness motivates our attitude to offer these acts to God without any claim for praise, appreciation or recognition of the service rendered.

### Principle 5: We take up Sai leadership as a sacred calling
At MySAIYa, leadership is a sacred calling of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba as a means for spiritual fulfilment. Therefore, contrary to the successful leaders of the world immersed in the self-centred pursuit of careers and cutthroat competition, a MySAIYa member will never give in to ego or anger, and they will never, ever resort to the ways of bureaucrats or dictators. True Sai leadership, in our eyes, transcends authoritative roles. Leadership, to us, isn't about guiding others but a spiritual practice for enhancing introspection and a service opportunity to develop selflessness, therefore necessitating the mental attitude of always keeping the welfare of others at the forefront. It holds to a Sai leader that selflessness is the very core of leadership.

Naturally, it is understood that a MySAIYa leader must encourage and inspire, and it is all about being humble, gentle, kind, loving, possessing sound character, consistency, and unwavering commitment. It's about braving the storms with grace and guiding others through adversity. Therefore instead of gaining control or assertion over others, leaders of MySAIYa aspire to be beacons of hope, illuminating paths with their positive attitudes, impeccable behaviour, and undying commitment to a larger purpose. We believe leaders who embody excellence inspire others, setting the tone for future generations.

Ideal MySAIYa leaders also say what they think (there is no duplicity in them) and do what they say (there is no insincerity or hypocrisy in their deeds). In brief, they are transparent and straightforward people in word and conduct.

Our activities, events, and initiatives aren't mere undertakings but reflections of this commitment to unparalleled quality and outstanding contributions as we commit ourselves to making a tangible difference for a better world as part of our spiritual endeavour. To do this, we harness our skills and talents to create impactful experiences, ensuring that we guide and support our community in navigating life's intricacies and staying anchored in time-tested spiritual principles.

### Principle 6: We pursue human excellence
Our pursuit of excellence is embedded in a deeper understanding of human values, moral virtues, personal principles, and skills. It's about consistently striving for self-transformation, achieving self-mastery, and becoming torchbearers of values. With love in our hearts, we believe that excellence is not unattainable or a daunting pursuit; in contrast, it is a beacon for growth and learning.

Part of striving for excellence includes the need to be professional. However, in the realm of MySAIYa, professionalism does not refer to dressing sense or ability to converse in English or some distorted idea of quality, but our professionalism melds seamlessly with humanness. Therefore, dedication, integrity, and commitment are not mere words but the lifeblood of our ethos. We believe that one must evolve into an exemplary human being before becoming a stellar professional. This intertwining of self-empowerment and professional development is a testament to our commitment.

In a world where excellence can be misconstrued as superiority or greatness, leading to pompousness or egoism, our belief in human values and the teachings of Sathya Sai Baba is our sanctuary. Therefore our drive for excellence is not connected or comparable with pompous greatness or an overt sense of superiority, but it arises from a place of humility and underscores the age-old wisdom that virtue isn't just about inner goodness but is manifested through our actions and deeds, which is genuinely the excellence that we aim for.

Therefore, it is essential to know that at MySAIYa, we're not chasing greatness or perfection but embracing excellence. This isn't a race for accolades; it's a journey of becoming, of being driven by values like honesty, compassion, and integrity. The purpose? To evolve, to learn, to inspire. It is the compass guiding our every action, intention, and thought, steering us away from the superficial allure of mere greatness, financial allure or perfection. Excellence, in our eyes, isn't about fleeting achievements but an ongoing commitment to be the best versions of ourselves in every situation. This 'best' isn't about superiority or competition but is firmly rooted in the human values of love, peace, non-violence, righteousness and truth. It's a call to arms, challenging us to continuously reach for our highest potential, not for accolades or commendations, but to lead fulfilling and meaningful lives.

Another aspect of excellence is our lifelong pursuit of knowledge and learning. We believe that the journey of spirituality is a lifelong one and that we should always be open to learning new things. Therefore we are committed to seeking knowledge, understanding, and wisdom in order to grow as individuals and as a team. This principle ensures we evolve spiritually, adapting to new learnings and perspectives as we grow. We understand spiritual advancement is not instantaneous; it requires continuous introspection, devotion, selfless service, discipline, and personal development. It is best achieved through an integrated practice of devotion, study, and service. Therefore, as Sai Young Adults, when we undertake a path of continuous learning, seeking knowledge, understanding, and wisdom, we commit to reading and reflecting on what we have read but, more importantly, we strive to regularly apply what we have learnt in our daily lives.

### Principle 7: We embrace spirituality with fun and joy
Our commitment to fun and joy is a testament to the belief that spirituality should not be burdensome but a source of delight. We strive to design activities and programs that uplift the spirit and bring a sense of playfulness, enthusiasm, creativity and social bonding. By fostering an atmosphere of joy and laughter, we create spaces where young individuals can experience the beauty of spirituality in a lighthearted and engaging manner.

As we embrace the spiritual, fun, interesting, appealing, and professional aspects of youth engagement, we remain ever-mindful of the transformative power of Swami's teachings. Offering unique or exclusive experiences can attract young adults, as these opportunities often generate social media shares and discussions among their peers. Accordingly, we attempt to recognise and reward their contributions to motivate continued engagement.

To resonate with young adults, we craft programs addressing their challenges and aspirations, staying attuned to the changing world while anchoring in spiritual principles. The very appeal of our existence lies in our relevance to the lives of young adults.

To stay relevant to the ever-changing needs of young adults, we try to be understanding of the challenges and aspirations they face in today's fast-paced world. Then, through appropriate workshops, retreats, and discussions, we offer practical tools and insights that help young souls find balance, purpose, and fulfilment.

### Principle 8: We pledge first to be, then do, then tell
In the journey of Sathya Sai leadership, we, as MySAIYa leaders embrace the profound principle emphasised by Swami "First to be, then do, and only then tell." This principle is deeply rooted in the teachings of Sathya Sai Baba, who inspires us to embody a standard of living that transcends worldly expectations. It's about nurturing our inner selves first, ensuring our actions align with our spiritual beliefs, and sharing our experiences and wisdom with others. This reflects our commitment to authentic leadership in the Sathya Sai fold.

Our commitment to this principle begins with self-nourishment. Like trees drawing life from their roots, we draw our spiritual strength from daily practices such as Namasmarana, engaging with spiritual content weekly, maintaining dietary discipline, and practising kindness, compassion, and generosity. These practices are not mere activities; they are the essence of our spiritual sustenance, keeping us grounded and spiritually robust.

However, the journey doesn't stop at self-improvement. It's crucial to translate our inner growth into actions. We strive to embody the values we cherish – respect and honour for our parents and teachers, adherence to the Nine Codes of Conduct and the Ten Guiding Principles of SSIOM, and respectful interactions in all circumstances. This alignment of our inner beliefs and outward actions sets the stage for authentic leadership.

Finally, we share our message. But this sharing comes with a deep understanding that words are powerful yet fleeting. Our credibility as leaders hinges on the consistency between what we say and what we do. In a world where actions speak louder than words, our leadership is defined by our guidance and example. We recognise the pitfalls of hypocrisy and strive to be leaders who not only 'talk the talk' but 'walk the walk.' This alignment of belief, action, and communication is the essence of "Be, Do, Tell" leadership – a commitment to living and leading with authenticity, integrity, and trust.

In embracing this principle, we acknowledge the challenges and the profound growth it brings. It's a journey of continuous self-reflection, learning, and evolution, where we not only inspire others through our words but, more importantly, through our actions and the authenticity of our character.

### Principle 9: We commit to the Sai collective discipline
Foremost is our stance on non-solicitation of funds. We maintain a clear boundary from financial transactions, emphasising selfless service and devotion over monetary contributions. This discipline reinforces our dedication to spiritual wealth over material gains.

Gender separation in our meetings and activities is another aspect of our discipline. This practice allows young men and women to focus on their spiritual growth and provides a safe space for discussing gender-sensitive topics. It reflects our respect for individual spiritual journeys and our commitment to creating an environment conducive to personal growth.

Communication within our community is governed by kindness and respect. We avoid harsh language, choosing instead to speak in a soft and obliging manner. This discipline extends to our punctuality and respect for the timing of activities, reflecting our respect for each other's time and commitment.

Our principle of non-proselytization is crucial, especially given Malaysia's multicultural and multi-religious context. We focus on the unity of all religions, encouraging members to explore their spiritual paths within their own traditions while being mindful of the sensitivities surrounding the Islamic faith.

Dietary discipline is another cornerstone of our collective practice. Observing a strictly vegetarian diet during our gatherings symbolises our commitment to non-violence and respect for all life forms. This discipline extends to refraining from substance abuse, alcohol, smoking, and tobacco consumption, ensuring that our gatherings are in harmony with the purity and sanctity of our spiritual objectives.

Our attire reflects the 'Sai culture' – modest, decent, and clean. We adhere to specific guidelines for clothing during prayer sessions and workshops, emphasising purity and simplicity. For women, the requirement of appropriate coverings ensures modesty and respect for our cultural values. While men’s white cloths reflect the commitment to belong to the spiritual fold and purity. The choice of materials and styles for clothing is guided by the principles of modesty and appropriateness, avoiding any form of outlandish or revealing attire.

In embracing these disciplines, we are not merely following rules but expressing our collective commitment to a higher spiritual purpose. These disciplines are expressions of our devotion, respect for each other, and dedication to the path laid out by Sathya Sai Baba. They are the tangible expressions of our inner spiritual commitment, helping us to stay grounded, united, and focused on our spiritual journey.

### Principle 10: Adherence to guidelines
It is understood that young adults join MySAIYa for self-improvement; therefore, it is agreed that all members of MySAIYa are works in progress and, in their capacity, rate and enthusiasm are threading on the path of improvement.

Therefore, the goal is to start putting into practice the expected Sadhana to one’s best ability and build on that effort consistently. The most straightforward expectation is to work on the Nine-Point Code of Conduct and the Ten Guiding Principles by Bhagawan Sri Sathya Baba.

No stringent monitoring mechanism is in place to evaluate this compliance. However, the effort of one’s own accord is expected. MySAIYa members must remain motivated to become exemplars of Sathya Sai teachings.

#### A simple set of Sadhana to abide by the Adherence to:

2. Bhajan or Prayer with family members at least once a week.

7. Curb Jealousy, Hatred and Envy.

9. Observe your Country’s Laws and be Exemplary Citizens.
10. Adore God, Abhor Sin.

## Part B: MySAIYa Codes of Practice

### Code 1: Respectful interaction with everyone at all times
In the heart of MySAIYa's ethos lies our first and foremost principle: Respectful interactions with everyone at all times. This principle is the bedrock of our conduct and interactions, reflecting our commitment to fostering a community grounded in love, respect, and unity.

- **Politeness and Kind Communication:** We emphasise speaking softly, lovingly, and obligingly. Our words should reflect our inner serenity and respect for others. In every situation, our speech should be imbued with kindness and understanding.
- **Proper Greetings and Designations:** A smile is our universal greeting, symbolising warmth and acceptance. We address each other as 'brother', 'sister', 'uncle', 'aunty', or with local honorifics like 'anna' or 'akka', reinforcing our bond as a spiritual family. This practice extends to newcomers and external audiences, embodying our inclusive spirit.
- **Courtesy in Simple Gestures:** The use of 'please' and 'thank you' in our interactions is not just about manners; it's about showing appreciation and respect for the efforts of others.
- **Respecting Personal Space:** We are mindful of personal boundaries and always seek consent before entering someone's personal space. This respect extends to being considerate of people's privacy and comfort.
- **Active and Respectful Listening:** A good listener is as important as speaking. We pay full attention to others, valuing their opinions and ensuring a balanced and respectful dialogue.
- **Mindful Use of Technology:** We recognise the importance of being present in our interactions. During bhajans, meetings, and talks, we put away electronic devices to fully engage with the experience and the people around us.

#### Avoid the following disrespectful behaviours at all times:
- **Disruptive Behavior:** We firmly discourage angry outbursts, verbal threats, bullying, and any form of physical confrontation. Our community is a safe space, free from abusive language and physical violence.
- **Demeaning Behavior:** Acts of shaming, personal insults, spiteful behaviour, and spreading rumours are contrary to our values. We strive to maintain the dignity of every individual, avoiding any behaviour that could be perceived as demeaning or hurtful.
- **Insensitive to Diversity:** We are committed to an environment free from misogynistic, racist, or sexually explicit comments. Our interactions are devoid of sexual harassment or innuendo, and we refrain from making personal comments on appearance, age, or private life.
- **Nonverbal Insidious Behavior:** We recognise that disrespectful behaviour can also be nonverbal. Therefore, we discourage lewd gestures, uncomfortable staring, overbearing attitudes, and exclusionary body language. Sarcasm, taunting, and hostile written communications are also seen as disrespectful. We advocate for maintaining a respectful personal space and encourage collaborative and inclusive behaviour.
- **Malicious Online-Offline Behavior:** We extend our respectful interactions to the digital realm. Unwanted invitations, sharing explicit messages or images without consent, and engaging in offensive online discussions are strictly prohibited. We emphasise the importance of digital respect and privacy, ensuring that our online behaviour aligns with our spiritual values and community standards.

### Code 2: Advocate unity and avoidance of conflict
As young adults who are committed to truth, love, morality, and integrity and strive to bring a good name to their parents, the organisation, and Swami, they should not be influenced, affected, or involved in differences of opinion and conflicts that may arise with adults of Sai Centres or between Sai Centres.

Young adults shall always project Swami's highest ideal of universal love, brotherhood of man, and fatherhood of God, which is that of 'Unity is Divinity'. Therefore, they will refrain from getting entangled in conflicts and instead focus on their spiritual growth and contributing positively to society. We will love and respect all our Sai uncles and aunties, brothers and sisters and children wholeheartedly under ALL circumstances.

Similarly, we also strive to keep the non-proselytisation culture of Sai alive and robust. We share content that emphasises the unity of all religions and encourages followers to explore their spiritual journeys within their respective traditions.

But when we speak about Sathya Sai Baba, we are not trying to convert others into Sri Sathya Sai Baba followers. We simply share His Universal message on human values with hopes of inspiring self-transformation and helping as many people as possible advance in their spiritual consciousness.

- **Upholding the reputation of Swami and the organisation under all circumstances:** We will bring a good name to our parents, the organisation, and Swami.
- **Love and respect our elders, peers and mentees**
- **Commitment to truth, love, morality and integrity:** We uphold the truth. We will manifest divine love and strive for the highest morality and integrity with peace and courage in our thoughts, words and deeds.

### Code 3: Maintain gender segregation
MySAIYa is a nationwide platform for people of diverse cultures, beliefs, and practices. Therefore, it is imperative to strike a balance to ensure that MySAIYa remains a safe, welcoming and inclusive space in the face of this diversity. This coincides with the primary aim of MySAIYa, being a catalyst for spiritual growth, one that happens in an environment devoid of distractions already prevalent in many spheres of our lives.

Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba’s explanation for this emphasises yet again the organisation's role as an enabler for personal growth and spiritual progress. Therefore, there is a need to keep the avenues as conducive as possible for focusing on the goal.

As part of a collective discipline practice, males and females of all age groups sit separately in Sai gatherings, meetings, Bhajan sessions, prayers, etc. This practice extends especially with more emphasis on young adults.

Besides, aspirants and young adults are more confident about sharing and healing their vulnerable factors within the same gender circle. In that circle, a sense of security prevails as they can behave as they please, share their ideas, and converse freely. There is a more relaxed atmosphere. This gender-segregation is also easier for those youths who have suffered from any kind of sexual assault. Therefore, some activities, programs and retreats (e.g. Yuvan and Yuvathi sessions, Rise for VIP) are conducted separately to enable young men and women to concentrate on their spiritual processes and to give them a safe space to discuss and address gender-sensitive topics.

As an extension of this rule, Sai young adults in MySAIYa gatherings, camps, at Sai centres, or while representing MySAIYa are prohibited from engaging in unwanted physical contact and inappropriate interactions such as patting, pinching, stroking, kissing, hugging, fondling, etc. This also includes putting a hand on the shoulder, grazing something while walking, standing uncomfortably close, and any other touch that makes the person uncomfortable.

### Code 4: Punctuality and Timing
Keeping Swami as an example, the Sai culture has always adhered to a rigorous schedule. Being ‘on time’ or ‘punctual’ means being able to arrive, complete tasks and meet obligations at or by a designated time.

### Code 5: Non-Proselytisation
We share content that emphasises the unity of all religions and encourages followers to explore their spiritual journeys within their respective traditions.

But when we speak about Sathya Sai Baba, we are not trying to convert others into Sri Sathya Sai Baba followers, but simply share His Universal message on human values with hopes to inspire self-transformation and helping as many people as possible to advance in their spiritual consciousness.

Respecting the country’s law and the sensitivity of most Muslims in Malaysia, we do not share any posts, facts, contents, etc., regarding the Islamic faith. We also adhere to the current law that prohibits Non-Muslims from the usage of certain Islamic terms in our writings, prayers, and Bhajans.

### Code 6: Dietary discipline
Observing a vegetarian diet, especially in MySAIYa events. Eating non-vegetarian food, including eggs, while gathering in Sathya Sai's or MySAIYa's name is strictly prohibited (even if it is a casual outing with Sai friends to a mall or catching up at a Mamak, etc.).

Substance abuse, consumption of alcohol, smoking, and chewing of tobacco and its related products are strictly prohibited while gathering in the name of Sathya Sai or MySAIYa.

### Code 7: Modest, decent and clean dress code
In Malaysia's rapidly evolving society, the dress code within Sai organisations, particularly among young adults, is a subject of much discussion. Our dress code ethos, centred on modesty, decency, and cleanliness, reflects our deep respect and love for Sri Sathya Sai Baba.

However, the interpretation of modesty and decency varies significantly across different socio-cultural backgrounds, especially in a multicultural and multiracial society like Malaysia. Generally, important aspects of modesty in most cultures refer to loose clothing, comfortable dressing and covering of the body to ensure not much skin is revealed.

The diversity in Sai organisation and Malaysian society underscores the importance of a dress code that promotes equality and inclusion, aligning with our spiritual mission, where everyone is equally valued.

Our commitment to a specific dress code is not about adhering to outdated norms but about fostering a sense of unity and selflessness, especially in service activities. It's crucial that both the providers and recipients of our service feel a sense of equality, minimising any sense of ego or superiority. Moreover, our appearance is critical in building trust within the organisation and the broader society, including the conservative majority. It also shapes perceptions about the organisation and Sathya Sai's teachings.

As an inclusive organisation that welcomes individuals from various backgrounds and walks of life, our dress code guidelines create a safe and conducive environment for everyone to experience and benefit from our offerings.

Despite occasional criticisms from some urban young adults that our dress code may seem archaic, it is inspired by the thousands of Western devotees at Prashanthi Nilayam who embrace sarees and kurtas, demonstrating that adherence to these guidelines supports spiritual growth rather than restriction. Prashanthi Nilayam's strict dress code, respected by devotees worldwide, serves as a reminder of the importance of following organisational guidelines.

This being said, we should not take it upon ourselves to enforce the dress code upon anyone else, especially visitors, newcomers, or those unaware of the guidelines. As people become familiar with Sathya Sai, they will slowly start to adapt themselves to the ways of Sai.

Meanwhile, for the rest, it is imperative to approach dress code inconsistencies with compassion and understanding. Addressing dress code issues should always be done respectfully and empathetically, preserving the dignity of the individual involved. If necessary, seek guidance from a senior MySAIYa member or a suitable devotee to convey the message appropriately.

It is particularly important to avoid overzealous or judgmental attitudes that could alienate newcomers, contradicting the inclusive spirit of Sai teachings. Remarks on dress codes should only be made by members of the same gender to maintain sensitivity and appropriateness. In all interactions, we are reminded of Swami’s teaching; while we may not always oblige, we can always speak obligingly, ensuring that our approach encourages rather than discourages spiritual engagement. This approach to the dress code reflects our dedication to creating an environment that is respectful, inclusive, and conducive to spiritual growth for all members of the MySAIYa community.

#### General Rules for All Members
These guidelines aim to ensure that all members of the MySAIYa community feel included, respected, and comfortable, promoting an atmosphere conducive to spiritual growth and service.
- **Modesty and Decency:** Members are expected to wear modest, decent, and clean attire that reflects respect and love for Sri Sathya Sai Baba. Clothing should neither attract undue attention nor distract others. This includes avoiding immodest, ostentatious clothing, unorthodox hairstyles, and flashy accessories.
- **Appropriate Symbols and Messages:** Garments with inappropriate symbols, messages, vulgarities, or logos representing sports teams, music bands, political parties, or contradictory NGOs are prohibited. If such logos are present, they should be covered up.
- **Comfort and Cleanliness:** Clothes should be clean and comfortable, ensuring they are appropriate for the setting and activities involved.
- **Prohibited Attire:** Wearing shorts (including three-quarter pants), sleeveless garments, transparent, semi-transparent, tight-fitting or body-hugging attire, and beachwear during any Sai activity is strictly prohibited.

#### MySAIYa Sisters - Specific Guidelines
##### General Attire
MySAIYa sisters are encouraged to dress in a way that is modest and respectful, without the necessity to adhere strictly to Indian dressing customs, especially if they are of non-Indian origin. The principal practice is to dress to the ankles and use a dupatta/shawl over the upper torso, except when wearing a saree or during sports/service activities requiring physical exertion.

##### During Sports or Service Activities
- Use the official MySAIYa or SSIOM T-shirt where available.
- Avoid body-hugging, sleeveless, and very transparent materials. Garments should conceal the back and shoulders.
- As a rough guide, necklines should be kept within 4 finger-widths of your collarbone.
- Opt for pants that are not too tight or revealing but not too large or saggy.
- Avoid see-through materials.

##### Virtual Calls and Online Meetings
Comfortable shirts or blouses are acceptable, but a shawl or scarf across the upper torso that covers the skin around the neck, chest, and shoulders is mandatory.

##### Specific Attire Types
- **Sarees:** Avoid sleeveless and low-neckline blouses. Ensure the front and back, including the waist area, are not exposed. The saree should be draped neatly and elegantly.
- **Salwar-Kameez:** Must be worn with a shawl across the upper torso (avoid hanging the shawl over one shoulder as it does not meet the modesty requirement).
- **Western cloth or Chinese traditional:** Ensure long pants or slacks are not too tight, and blouses cover the entire lower hip section. If you prefer skirts, ensure hemlines are at least 3 inches below the knee. Use a scarf or shawl across the upper torso.

#### MySAIYa Brothers - Specific Guidelines
- White shirts and white pants are preferred. The shirt is usually worn outside the pants.
- A white dhoti can be worn.
- A short, neat and well-groomed hair is expected.

### Code 8: Sai Centre participation
Aligned with the philosophy of selfless service (Seva) taught by Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, MySAIYa encourages young adults to engage in service activities that benefit communities and the environment, fostering a sense of responsibility and kinship with all life.

Actively participate in activities at all levels of the organisation. As a rough guide to benchmark one’s commitment to seeing the adequacy in being engaged with the Sai mission, at least four hours a week is recommended to be allocated for organisational activities of any level. MySAIYa provides a variety of spiritual activities, such as study circles, meditation sessions, and devotional singing (Bhajans) that allow young adults to explore and deepen their spiritual practices in a supportive community setting.

### Code 9: Non-solicitation
In alignment with the non-solicitation culture of SSIOM, MySAIYa maintains financial integrity through transparent accounting and ethical stewardship of resources. All financial dealings are recorded and made accessible to members per the stipulated guidelines of SSIOM.

Non-solicitation for funds of any kind (to have little to do with money), especially from the public. This is applied by:
- Refraining from requesting or promoting any financial solicitations (especially on behalf of the organisation).
- Promoting the value of selfless service and devotion over monetary contributions.

#### DISAPPROVED means of fund generating deemed as a severe misdemeanour of MySAIYa:
- Solicitation of donations or contributions in any way, especially by posters, messages, circular letters and adverts to the general public for donations by using the name of Sathya Sai or SSIOM or MySAIYa.
- Using online platforms, crowdfunding sites or social media to appeal for funds or investments for any project under Sathya Sai, SSIOM, MySAIYa or local Sai Centre.
- Organising, co-organising or participating in any public events under Sathya Sai, SSIOM, MySAIYa or local Sai Centre to raise funds.
- Persuasion or compulsion of any Sai devotee or well-wisher to donate to any SAI activity.
- Organising dinners, fun fairs and jumble sales, dance shows, musical shows or any form of program in the name of Sathya Sai to raise funds.
- Involvement in any charity fundraising activities with other NGOs or Government ministries.

#### Acceptable Means (BUT DISCOURAGED to be pursued as an advocation or solicitation):
- **Direct unsolicited donations:** Devotees or well-wishers wanting to donate on their own accord without any persuasion, compulsion or solicitation for various reasons (touched by the impact, want to be part of the mission, religious obligation, etc.). Members must direct them to the SSIOM Council treasurer team, as MySAIYa members (including the committee) should not receive funds on behalf of the organisation. All transactions must have their receipts. Alternatively, provide bank details of the Council.
- **Legally assigned assets:** Devotees and well-wishers may voluntarily assign their savings, insurance policies or assets via a legally executed "WILL" or assignment (applicable only for SSIOM, Centre, and Bhajan unit levels, not MySAIYa directly).
- **Government grants:** Where government grants, programs or public funds are available, they may be obtained with the endorsement of the National Young Adult Coordinator (who is responsible for informing and forwarding details to the Council Secretariat for approval). The National Young Adult Coordinator will be responsible for ensuring the maintenance of proper accounting records and having them audited before submitting the relevant documents and accounts to the relevant authority with a copy to the Council secretariat within the prescribed timeline.
- **Member transactions:** Financial transactions for camps, T-shirt sales, Sai or spiritual and other religious literature sales, pictures of Sai and souvenir items sold to fellow MySAIYa members or Sai devotees and friends. They should be reasonably priced to cover costs; profit should not be the motive, regardless of intent.
- **Recycling programs:** Recycling programs can be organised to promote conservation awareness, and profits from that can be used if necessary (however, organizing a recycling campaign for fundraising with environmental reasons secondary is not allowed).
- **Altar donation boxes:** Given the mobile nature of our physical meetups, no donation box representing MySAIYa should be displayed, made known prominently or advertised at all near the altar.
- **Distributed workshop costs:** Programs such as camps or workshops for Sai devotee young adults and teens may incur costs that can be distributed among the Sai devotee benefactors. The Council Secretariat must be informed of the budget plan.
- **Ceiling on Desire:** MySAIYa Committee members are encouraged to save through Ceiling on Desire intent, as pointed out by Swami in the Nine Codes of Conduct, and use the savings for planned intended service activities.

## Part C: General Guidelines & Moderation

### Treat Everyone With Respect
- **You may:** Disagree respectfully, Ask questions, Offer alternative perspectives, Engage in constructive discussion.
- **You may not:** Insult, mock, humiliate, harass, intimidate, or attack the character, background, beliefs, or identity of another user.

### Celebrate Diversity
MySAIYa welcomes people from different races, cultures, faith traditions, professions, and backgrounds. Respectful interfaith discussions are encouraged; religious superiority arguments are not.

### Be Helpful
Ask yourself: Does this contribute positively? Good examples include:
- Sharing experiences
- Offering encouragement
- Answering questions
- Supporting newcomers
- Celebrating achievements

### Keep Discussions Constructive
Criticism is acceptable when it is respectful, solution-focused, and addresses ideas rather than people. Personal attacks are never acceptable.

### Zero-Tolerance Behaviour
The following may result in immediate removal or suspension:
- Harassment or bullying
- Hate speech or threats
- Doxxing or impersonation
- Fraud or scams
- Sexual exploitation
- Violence promotion or self-harm encouragement

### Commercial Activity
MySAIYa is a volunteer-driven platform. Users may not advertise businesses, sell products, recruit for commercial schemes, or conduct unauthorised fundraising without written approval.

### Protect Privacy
Never share private conversations, personal contact information, confidential committee discussions, or internal documents without consent.

### Gamification & Leaderboards
Leaderboards, badges, XP, and achievements are designed to encourage participation—not competition for ego. Users should:
- Celebrate others
- Encourage participation
- Maintain humility
- Focus on growth rather than rankings

Attempts to manipulate scores, exploit systems, or gain unfair advantages may result in account restrictions.

### Moderation
To maintain a safe environment, moderators may review, hide, or remove content, restrict interactions, suspend accounts, or permanently ban users when necessary. Moderation decisions are guided by community safety, platform integrity, and organisational values.

### Reporting Concerns
If you encounter content that may violate these guidelines, contact info@ssiomya.com with screenshots, URLs, dates, and a brief explanation. Please avoid public arguments or retaliation. Allow moderators to investigate fairly.

### Our Digital Sai Principle
Before posting, commenting, uploading, sharing, reacting, or messaging, ask yourself:
- **Is it true?**
- **Is it kind?**
- **Is it respectful?**
- **Is it helpful?**

Does it bring people closer to love, unity, and understanding? If not, reconsider before posting.
