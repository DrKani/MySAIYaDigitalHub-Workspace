Welcome to MySAIYa's Digital Hub. We respect your rights under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA). This document outlines the structured workflow and form fields established for users requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data held by MySAIYa.

---

## Form Fields

### 1. Contact Information
* **Full Name** (Required, max 120 chars)
* **Email Address** (Required, max 254 chars)
* **Phone Number** (Required, max 30 chars)
* **MySAIYa Account Email** (Optional) - Registered account email, if different from above.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Required) - Dropdown selection (Email, Phone Call, WhatsApp Message)

### 2. Request details
* **Request Type** (Required) - Dropdown selection:
  * Access my personal data
  * Correct my information
  * Delete my account
  * Delete uploaded content
  * Export my personal data
  * Withdraw marketing consent
  * Withdraw media consent
  * Report privacy concern
  * Other
* **Description of Request** (Required, max 4000 chars) - Large text field to describe the specific data or correction details needed.

### 3. Verification & Documents
* **Identity Verification Checkbox** (Required) - "I confirm that I am the data subject or an authorised representative."
* **Supporting Documents** (Optional upload) - Attachment supporting the request (e.g. NRIC partially masked, authorization letter). Accepted formats: PDF, JPEG, PNG. Max size: 2MB.

---

## Expected Processing Time
Upon submission, requests are queued for administrative review. Personal data requests are normally resolved within **21–30 calendar days**, depending on request complexity. You will be notified via your preferred contact method once your request has been updated.
