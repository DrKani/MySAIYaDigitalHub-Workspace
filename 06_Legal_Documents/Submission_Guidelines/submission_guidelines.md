# MySAIYa Digital Hub — Content Submission Guidelines

**Effective Date:** 23rd November 2026

## Introduction
The MySAIYa Digital Hub ("the Site") is an online platform operated by the Malaysian Sai Young Adults (MySAIYa) under the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM).

### Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM)
The Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM) is a Malaysian non-profit organisation listed under the Societies Act 1966, with licence number PPM-001-14-10081983, and has been established and registered with the Registrar of Societies Malaysia (ROS) since October 1983. As a multifaceted spiritual movement with service as its core practice, SSIOM provides the platform for its multicultural, multi-religious and multiracial members to put into practice the inspiring teachings of one of the world's revered spiritual teachers, Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba through its vibrant mosaic of social welfare, humanitarian, traditional, devotional, youth development, philosophical, community improvements, educational outreach, cultural nourishment, and nation-building endeavours aimed for elevating the collective consciousness of the society.

### Malaysian Sai Young Adults (MySAIYa)
The Malaysian Sai Young Adults (MySAIYa) is a vibrant movement of volunteers aged 18 to 40 within the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM). United by the universal teachings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, we are a diverse community from many races, faiths, and cultural backgrounds committed to cultivating human values, uplifting society, strengthening national unity, and serving the poor, sick, and needy. Guided by the MySAIYa Committee under SSIOM's constitution, we strive to live with purpose, serve with love, and inspire a new generation of Malaysians to rise in human excellence—transcending all boundaries of religion, ethnicity, gender, and age.

### ACCESS RESTRICTION (MALAYSIAN LAW)
Please note that, in compliance with Article 11(4) of the Federal Constitution of Malaysia and relevant state enactments (such as Sections 5 and 6 of the Non-Islamic Religions (Control of Propagation Among Muslims) Enactment), registration to site membership and full engagement (e.g., commenting, user profiles) on this platform are limited exclusively to non-Muslim Malaysians. Muslim brothers and sisters interested in joining a spiritual community are respectfully encouraged to participate in activities within Islamic organisations and institutions across Malaysia. However, our community services, societal commitments, and general non-religious initiatives are available to all individuals, regardless of their religious beliefs, ethnicity, gender, or age.

Thank you for contributing to the MySAIYa Digital Hub. The strength of this platform lies in the collective wisdom, creativity, experiences, and service of its community members. These Content Submission Guidelines explain what content may be submitted, the standards expected of contributors, and how MySAIYa and the Sathya Sai International Organisation of Malaysia (SSIOM) may review, edit, publish, archive, or remove submitted content.

These guidelines apply to all content submitted through the Site, including but not limited to:
- Blog articles
- Event reports
- Reflections and testimonies
- Service stories
- Quiz questions and educational content
- Comments and discussion posts
- Member spotlight submissions
- Photos and videos
- Audio recordings
- Downloadable resources
- Forum discussions
- Polls and surveys
- User-generated content associated with games, leaderboards, badges, and community activities

---

## 1. Our Purpose
MySAIYa exists to inspire self-transformation through Service, Adoration, Illumination, Human values, Leadership, Lifelong learning, National unity, and Spiritual growth.
Every contribution should support one or more of these objectives.

Before submitting, ask yourself: **Does this content help someone become a better human being?** If the answer is yes, it likely belongs here.

---

## 2. Content We Welcome
We encourage submissions that are educational, uplifting, reflective, practical, inspiring, or community-focused. Examples include:
- **Spiritual Growth:** Reflections on Sai teachings, study circle insights, Namasmarana experiences, meditation, book reviews, personal journeys.
- **Service & Community:** Service project reports, volunteer experiences, humanitarian initiatives, environmental projects.
- **Personal Development:** Leadership lessons, communication, time management, youth development.
- **Culture & Heritage:** Festival explanations, cultural traditions, interfaith harmony, Malaysian heritage stories.
- **Creative Contributions:** Photography, artwork, graphic design, poetry, music, videos, infographics.
- **Educational Content:** Quizzes, learning modules, historical articles, human values resources.

---

## 3. Content Quality Standards
All submissions should be:
- **Truthful:** Avoid misinformation, speculation, or misleading claims.
- **Respectful:** Respect all individuals, communities, religions, and cultures.
- **Constructive:** Focus on solutions, learning, growth, and service.
- **Clear:** Use language that is understandable to a broad audience.
- **Inclusive:** Recognise the diversity of Malaysian society.
- **Relevant:** Ensure submissions align with MySAIYa's mission and values.

---

## 4. Originality & Intellectual Property
By submitting content, you confirm that:
- The content is your own work; or
- You possess all necessary rights and permissions.

Where applicable, credit original authors, cite sources, and obtain permission before using copyrighted materials.
You must not upload pirated content, copyrighted music/videos without permission, restricted organizational documents, or confidential information.

---

## 5. Photography & Media
Before submitting media ensure:
- You have permission from identifiable individuals.
- Event organisers have authorised sharing.
- Images reflect dignity and respect.

Particular care should be taken when featuring children, vulnerable individuals, or beneficiaries of service projects.

---

## 6. Privacy & Confidentiality
Do not publish NRIC/passport numbers, home addresses, phone numbers, financial info, medical records, or confidential organizational info. Protect the privacy of yourself and others.

---

## 7. Sensitive Topics
Personal experiences involving health, mental well-being, grief, or personal challenges should be shared respectfully, avoiding exposing third parties, sensationalism, or providing professional advice.

---

## 8. Prohibited Content
The following content is not permitted:
- **Harmful Content:** Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, discrimination.
- **Illegal Content:** Fraud, piracy, criminal activity.
- **Explicit Content:** Pornography, graphic violence.
- **Commercial Promotion:** Advertising, MLM schemes, unauthorised fundraising.
- **Political Advocacy:** Partisan messaging, campaigning.
- **Religious Proselytisation:** Content must not seek to pressure, recruit, or convert individuals from any faith.

---

## 9. Editorial Review
All submissions may be reviewed before publication. Moderators may correct grammar, improve formatting, add headings/metadata, optimise readability, or reject unsuitable submissions. Publication is not guaranteed.

---

## 10. Use of Submitted Content
By submitting content, you grant SSIOM and MySAIYa a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide licence to display, publish, archive, translate, adapt, and share the content for purposes aligned with the mission of MySAIYa and SSIOM. Attribution will be provided where reasonable.

---

## 11. Content Removal Requests
If you wish to remove previously submitted content, contact info@ssiomya.com with the title, URL/screenshot, and reason for removal. We will endeavour to accommodate requests.

---

## 12. Final Principle
Before submitting, ask:
- **Is it true?**
- **Is it kind?**
- **Is it helpful?**
- **Is it aligned with human values?**

Does it bring people closer to love, unity, and understanding? If so, we encourage you to share it.

---

## 13. MySAIYa Voices Contributor Guidelines

### MySAIYa Voices
**Share Your Story. Inspire Another Journey.**

*Om Sai Ram!*

Every Sai Young Adult has a story worth sharing.

Perhaps it was a service project that changed your perspective. Perhaps a retreat, satsang, study circle, leadership experience, or a simple moment that strengthened your faith and values.

MySAIYa Voices is a platform where Malaysian Sai Young Adults can share personal experiences, reflections, lessons, and inspirations with the wider MySAIYa community.

Your story may encourage someone facing similar challenges, inspire future volunteers, or preserve meaningful memories for generations of Sai Young Adults to come.

---

### What Can You Write About?
We welcome original articles based on your experiences as a Sai Young Adult. Possible topics include:

#### Personal Journey
- How you became involved in MySAIYa
- Important lessons learned
- Spiritual growth and self-transformation
- Experiences balancing Sai activities with studies, work, or family

#### Service Experiences
- Community service projects
- Volunteer initiatives
- Memorable interactions with beneficiaries
- Lessons learned through service

#### Events and Programmes
- Retreats and camps
- National conventions
- Study circles and satsangs
- Leadership programmes
- Cultural and spiritual activities

#### Challenges and Growth
- Obstacles you faced
- How Sai teachings helped you navigate difficulties
- Important turning points in your life
- Personal breakthroughs

#### Community Impact
- Positive changes within your centre or community
- Teamwork and collaboration experiences
- Inspiring people you have encountered

---

### Writing Guidelines

#### Article Length
- **Recommended:** 400–800 words
- **Maximum:** 1,000 words

#### Writing Style
Please write:
- ✅ **Authentically**
- ✅ **Positively**
- ✅ **Clearly and conversationally**
- ✅ **From personal experience**
- ✅ **In your own voice**

*Write as though you are sharing your experience with a friend.*

---

### What to Avoid
Please avoid:
- ❌ Internal administrative matters
- ❌ Centre disputes or conflicts
- ❌ Personal criticisms of individuals
- ❌ Complaints that should be directed through official channels
- ❌ Excessive jargon or highly technical language
- ❌ Content that is political, divisive, or inconsistent with SSIOM values

---

### Author Information
At the beginning of your article, include your Name and Sai Centre / Locality.

*Example:*  
**Kani, Johor Bahru.**  
This will be used for your byline.

---

### Photographs and Media
We encourage contributors to include photographs. Please provide:

#### Required Images
1. One portrait photograph
2. One or more activity photographs

#### Image Requirements
- JPEG or PNG format
- High resolution where possible
- Original images preferred

#### Captions
Provide a caption for each image, including:
- Names of individuals (where appropriate)
- Event or activity name
- Location
- Date (if relevant)

#### Photo Credits
Please identify the photographer or image source.

---

### Formatting Requirements
Please submit your article using Google Docs.

#### Document Formatting
| Item | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Font** | Open Sans |
| **Title** | Bold, 15pt |
| **Body Text** | 13pt |
| **Alignment** | Left-aligned |
| **Line Spacing** | Default |
| **Special Formatting** | Avoid excessive styling |

---

### Attachments
If your article includes photos, tables, charts, illustrations, or infographics:
- Place them at the end of the document.
- Within the article body, indicate placement using markers such as:
  - `<Insert Photo 1 Here>`
  - `<Insert Table 1 Here>`
  - `<Insert Illustration 1 Here>`

---

### Submission Process

- **Step 1:** Complete the MySAIYa Voices Submission Form.
- **Step 2:** Upload your Google Docs article, images, and supporting materials.
- **Step 3:** Include the following details:
  - Full Name
  - Sai Centre
  - Email Address
  - Contact Number

---

### Editorial Review
All submissions are reviewed by the MySAIYa Communications Team.

The team may make minor edits for:
- Grammar
- Spelling
- Formatting
- Consistency

Any substantial changes will be discussed with the author. The original voice and intent of the article will always be respected.

---

### Before You Submit
Ask yourself:
- [ ] Is my story authentic?
- [ ] Does it inspire, educate, or encourage others?
- [ ] Does it reflect the values of love, service, and self-transformation?
- [ ] Have I checked my spelling and grammar?
- [ ] Have I attached my photographs and captions?

*If yes, you’re ready to submit.*
