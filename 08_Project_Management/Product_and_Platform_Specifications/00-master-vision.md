# Master vision

MySAIYa Digital Hub is a calm, accessible and trusted digital home for Malaysian Sai Young Adults: public discovery first, followed by member learning, participation and accountable committee operations.

## Outcomes

- Help visitors understand MySAIYa and take a safe next step.
- Give members a useful, respectful personal hub.
- Support learning, service and community without exposing personal data.
- Give authorised committee members auditable operational tools.

## Guardrails

- Mobile-first, accessible and multilingual-ready.
- No role may be self-assigned to gain privileged access.
- Collect only information required for a stated purpose.
- Sacred, warm and contemporary—not gamified at the expense of clarity.

## Success measures

Public task completion, verified account completion, active learning participation, resolved submissions, accessibility conformance and zero unresolved critical security findings.
