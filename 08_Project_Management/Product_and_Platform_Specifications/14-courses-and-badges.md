# Courses and badges

Learning experiences should have a clear learning goal, age-appropriate content, progress model, completion evidence and accessible fallback. Badge awards must be deterministic, auditable and reversible by an administrator only when justified.

Track course content/version separately from user progress. Never make badges or streaks a condition of access to essential information or community participation. See `apps/web/docs/07-gamification-system.md` for existing direction.
