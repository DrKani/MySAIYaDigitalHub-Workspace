# Launch plan

## Phases

1. Public-site content, navigation and shared UI stabilised.
2. Authentication, permissions and role tests completed.
3. Limited internal pilot with support and incident contacts.
4. Content/operations readiness review, then phased public launch.
5. Post-launch monitoring, feedback triage and retrospective.

Before each phase: identify owner, scope, success metrics, support route, rollback decision-maker and go/no-go checklist. Publish only verified legal links, approved content and tested forms.
